When your itinerary arrives — the 10-minute intake

Everything on this page is blank on purpose. When the confirmed itinerary lands, work down this list once and the planner is done.

  1. Flights & dates (rows: flights, hotel-stay): enter the booking totals, the exact check-in/check-out dates and the number of billable nights. Update every daily row to match the real dates.
  2. Hotel extras (hotel-separate-charges): read the invoice. The 2026 MTA guideline PDF lists €1.50 per night for each guest aged 18+ (cap €22.50 per person per visit), while the current MTA webpage separately says charges apply to nights that “exceeded fifteen (15) nights.” Ask MTA/AX ODYCY how the wording applies to a short stay; a six-night €9 calculation is conditional, not a confirmed charge. Enter only the amount actually billed, and keep separately charged deposits, resort fees or taxes distinct.
  3. Event access (event-access): a separate 11 October listing-card capture and HLTV report record a “Free / Reserve a spot” offer, but the current extracted page text did not expose a fee table. This is not a current checkout total, ticket availability or your reservation. Do not assume €0; enter only the confirmed admission amount and any fee after checking your own checkout. Details: CS2 event guide.
  4. Airport transfers (airport-transfer-arrival / -departure) and bus rows (local-bus-and-cards, event-commute): MPT lists day-route singles at €2.50 summer / €2.00 winter, night/Tallinja Direct at €3.00 and TD1 at €3.50; for cards it lists €19 for 12 shareable journeys, €21/€27 for Explore Flex 4/7-day (whose offer grid includes Airport Direct and Special Services) and €25 for the 7-day Explore Adult card, which excludes tallinja Direct routes. The applicable day-route single fare for 13–19 October 2026 is not established by the published winter dates. Confirm the current fare and compare card terms with your actual rides, then enter the real receipt; no card or itinerary is assumed.
  5. Optional return transport (taxi-rideshare): keep this blank unless you choose a taxi/ride-hailing service or the dated MPT planner shows no suitable public-bus return after the confirmed gate and finish. One static Route 186 variant lists a 20:53 departure, but that is not a full-network search or a trip-day journey. No quote exists until you request one; compare a live provider or hotel-arranged quote against Transport Malta’s published maximum meter rates (the tariff page may be stale; see the transport guide).
  6. Food, activities and gaming (food-and-drink, activities-and-sightseeing, gaming-and-arcade): set one daily allowance and put the whole-party amount in the aggregate row, or itemise in the seven daily rows — never both for the same spending.
  7. Connectivity and money (mobile-connectivity, bank-cash-fees): enter the eSIM/SIM price you actually buy and your bank’s foreign-transaction fee as a percentage-based estimate on planned card spend.
  8. Insurance, passports and contingency (travel-insurance, passport-entry-costs, other-incidentals): enter real premium/purchase amounts, then leave a deliberate contingency figure.
  9. Finish: check the Difference card (actual minus budget), then press Download CSV backup. The planner is then a trip record as well as a plan.

Price references come from the transport guide; they are the operator’s published prices, not quotes for your specific journeys.

Paste your itinerary, fill your budget

When the itinerary and confirmations arrive, paste one item per line below. The planner finds the date and the amount, works out which row the item belongs to, and shows you exactly what it would fill before anything changes. It only fills a budget field that is still blank, and it never overwrites an amount you have already typed. Nothing is sent anywhere; the text stays in this browser tab.

Each line needs at least an amount. A date helps the planner choose the right row. Recognised date shapes: 2026-10-14, 14/10, 14 Oct, Oct 14. Recognised amount shapes: €5.00, 5.00, 5. Quick-use notes

  • Each line has a budget and actual amount, both in euros. Use whole-trip/whole-party totals consistently; if you prefer per-person amounts, note that basis and apply it to every row.
  • The reference links are for reconfirming details or choosing a provider; they are not price quotes. No amount is prefilled, even where another guide page reports a dated menu or admission label.
  • The event-access row is blank until your own ticket and checkout total are confirmed. A dated listing capture recorded a free reservation offer, but the current extracted page text did not expose the fee table; do not prefill €0. Use the taxi row only for a taxi/ride-hailing return you actually choose or need after checking the dated planner.
  • Use the transport guide, food shortlist, activities and events, and everyday guide to compare sourced options before entering your own amounts.
