A short intake checklist
  1. Flights & dates (rows: flights, hotel-stay): enter the booking totals, the exact check-in/check-out dates and the number of billable nights. Update every daily row to match the real dates.
  2. Hotel extras (hotel-separate-charges): read the invoice. The 2026 MTA guideline PDF lists €1.50 per night for each guest aged 18+ (cap €22.50 per person per visit), while the current MTA webpage separately says charges apply to nights that “exceeded fifteen (15) nights.” Ask MTA/AX ODYCY how the wording applies to a short stay; a six-night €9 calculation is conditional, not a confirmed charge. Enter only the amount actually billed, and keep separately charged deposits, resort fees or taxes distinct.
  3. Event access (event-access): the official ticketing listing now advertises the Thunderpick Finals as free with a “Reserve a spot” action, 18+, in person — reserve first, then enter €0 for admission or the actual amount charged (including any fee). A listing is not proof of your own reservation or of the attendee gate; the real event-day cost to budget is the late return, in the taxi-rideshare row. Details: CS2 event guide.
  4. Airport transfers (airport-transfer-arrival / -departure) and bus rows (local-bus-and-cards, event-commute): MPT lists day-route singles at €2.50 summer / €2.00 winter, night/Tallinja Direct at €3.00 and TD1 at €3.50; for cards it lists €19 for 12 shareable journeys, €21/€27 for Explore Flex 4/7-day (whose offer grid includes Airport Direct and Special Services) and €25 for the 7-day Explore Adult card, which excludes tallinja Direct routes. The applicable day-route single fare for 13–19 October 2026 is likely €2.50 on 13–18 October and €2.00 on 19 October (third-party 2026 season guides plus the official winter-start date; not operator-confirmed). Confirm the current fare and compare card terms with your actual rides, then enter the real receipt; no card or itinerary is assumed.
  5. Return transport (taxi-rideshare): budget the event evenings as taxi/ride-hailing returns — the venue days are listed to 22:30 and no bus or night route was verified to bring you back after the last listed departure. No quote exists until you open Bolt or eCabs in Malta: enter the live quote for one Qawra–Ta’ Qali trip and multiply by the event nights you actually attend, then compare it against Transport Malta’s published maximum meter rates (the tariff ceiling on the transport guide).
  6. Food, activities and gaming (food-and-drink, activities-and-sightseeing, gaming-and-arcade): set one daily allowance and put the whole-party amount in the aggregate row, or itemise in the seven daily rows — never both for the same spending.
  7. Connectivity and money (mobile-connectivity, bank-cash-fees): enter the eSIM/SIM price you actually buy and your bank’s foreign-transaction fee as a percentage-based estimate on planned card spend.
  8. Insurance, passports and contingency (travel-insurance, passport-entry-costs, other-incidentals): enter real premium/purchase amounts, then leave a deliberate contingency figure.
  9. Finish: check the Difference card (actual minus budget), then press Download CSV backup. The planner is then a trip record as well as a plan.

Paste your itinerary, fill your budget

When the itinerary and confirmations arrive, paste one item per line below. The planner suggests a row from simple keywords and shows you the amount it would fill before anything changes. It only fills a budget field that is still blank, and it never overwrites an amount you have already typed. Nothing is sent anywhere; the text stays in this browser tab.

Use date | description | amount in EUR, or a description followed by €amount. Bare numbers without separators are not interpreted as prices. Explicit foreign currencies, negative amounts and ambiguous thousands separators need manual entry after conversion. Deposits and payment holds are never quick-filled: review them manually and keep refundable holds in notes. Multiple accepted lines for the same blank category are summed; existing amounts are never overwritten. Recognised date shapes: 2026-10-14, 14/10, 14 Oct, Oct 14. Recognised amount shapes: €5.00, 5.00, 5. Recognised keywords include flight, hotel, insurance, airport, taxi, bus, pass, food, meal, dinner, breakfast, coffee, museum, tour, ticket, sim, esim, roaming, atm, laundry and souvenir.

Quick-use notes

  • Each line has a budget and actual amount, both in euros. Use whole-trip/whole-party totals consistently; if you prefer per-person amounts, note that basis and apply it to every row.
  • The reference links are for reconfirming details or choosing a provider; they are not price quotes. No amount is prefilled, even where another guide page reports a dated menu or ticket price.
  • Use the event-access row only for your confirmed admission or booking fee, including zero if confirmed. A published offer is not your reservation; food and travel belong in their own rows.
  • Use the transport guide, food shortlist, activities and events, and everyday guide to compare sourced options before entering your own amounts.
