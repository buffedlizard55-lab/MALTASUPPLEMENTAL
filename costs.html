3The tax on a “free” trip (US rules, primary sources)

  • A won trip is taxable income. IRS Publication 525: prizes and awards from a drawing or contest must be included in income at fair market value (goods/services — for you: flight + hotel + ticket). Report on Form 1040, Schedule 1 line 8i. Its other key line: “If you refuse to accept a prize, don't include its value in your income.”
  • California concurs: the FTB's gambling/income page exempts only California Lottery winnings; everything else flows through federal AGI onto your CA return. Expect combined federal + CA at roughly your marginal rate on the FMV (≈ 24–32% for most earners — Estimate; a CPA should confirm the exact filing treatment of a non-cash travel prize).
  • FMV inputs: airfare (market $611–963 RT, mid-Oct sampling) + hotel (~€100–180/night × 7) + ticket value → a plausible FMV band of roughly $1,700–2,900 → tax ≈ $420–780. The calculator uses a default FMV you can adjust.
  • Paperwork: for prizes paid in 2026 the 1099-MISC information-reporting threshold is $2,000 (IRS 2026 instructions) — that's sponsor reporting, not an exemption, and a foreign promoter may not issue one to you at all. Keep the itinerary, any prize letter, and booking confirmations; a CPA is cheap insurance for the filing question.

Official IRS Pub 525 (Taxable and Nontaxable Income) · IRS Topic 419 (fair market value of prizes) · California FTB gambling income page · IRS 1099-MISC/NEC instructions (2026) — $2,000 prize threshold. Estimate the dollar bands are this site's arithmetic on those rules, not an IRS position.

6The $30/day food reality check

$30/day (≈€26) is tight but doable — with discipline
  • A sit-down dinner in Sliema or St Julian's runs €20–30 before drinks (consistent across TripAdvisor price bands for the island's mid-range restaurants).
  • The pattern that makes $30 work: pastizzi + coffee breakfast (€3–5), ftira/café lunch (€6–10), one mid-range dinner or a big lunch (€10–15).
  • Budget the high band ($300–375 for food) if you want one Top-10-guide dinner and one seafood night — the guide restaurants are €25–40+ per person.
