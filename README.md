The traveler supplied “BLAST Arena Studios, Attard” as the event destination. A Hotspawn Eventbrite listing read 11 October does confirm a venue address and map pin for that destination, but it is **not** confirmation of this traveller’s ticket, of current inventory, of the attendee gate inside the site, or of the weekend agenda.

## Key findings and limits

- **Event, venue and ticket listing:** A Hotspawn Eventbrite page read 11 October advertised an in-person, 18+ event at BLAST Arena Studios Malta, MFCC South Gate National Stadium, ATD 4000 Attard, 14–18 October, with a free “Reserve a spot” action. It listed check-in/games for Wed 14 (09:30/10:30, agenda to 22:30), Thu 15 (09:30/10:30, agenda to 22:30), and Fri 16 (14:00/15:00, agenda to 21:00); Sat/Sun agenda entries were not visible. The listing headline gives 22:00 while Wed/Thu rows extend to 22:30, so confirm exact finish times. This is dated listing evidence, not proof of current ticket inventory, the traveler’s reservation, per-day scanning or the attendee gate. Thunderpick’s official page gives the wider 12–19 October operational window; broadcast streams are not radio.
- **Event-day return risk:** One static Route 186 variant lists a 20:53 departure from the Ta’ Qali Stadium area toward Buġibba; the visible Eventbrite agenda for 14–16 October ends later (22:30 Wed/Thu, 21:00 Fri). That comparison flags a risk; it does not prove that no later or alternative bus runs. Route 186’s return does not list a Qawra stop and may need a separately checked onward leg. Route 214 lists Qawra stop 952 and Ta’ Qali Qali 2 stop 2542 on its static route page, making it a same-route candidate to test; TQ1 special event service is another possibility with no published TWC timetable. Check the date-specific planner, confirmed gate and current service updates; get a live taxi/ride-hailing quote as a fallback if no suitable return appears.
- **Event-day transport and fares:** Route 186 is a bus candidate between Qawra stop 952 and Ta’ Qali Stadium 6227; the return uses opposite-side Stadium stop 2194 toward Buġibba, not Qawra seafront. A second boarding or a walk may be needed; the dated onward connection and safe walks are unverified. Route 214 is another route-level candidate to check; MPT’s 17 October Route 186 notice is 19:00–00:00 on both current operator pages, while a separate older 20:00–00:00 notice ends around 21 April and is not a conflicting October window. The official fare table lists €2.50 summer / €2.00 winter singles, but its only stated winter validity ends 13 June 2026; the applicable 13–19 October 2026 fare is not established. Card prices/terms are comparisons, not an assumed purchase. Bolt is documented in Malta; sources conflict on Uber.
- **Special-event service to monitor:** MPT runs **TQ-numbered special event services** out of Ta’ Qali for major events, announced only days beforehand, and **TQ1’s published corridor runs through St Paul’s Bay and Qawra to Buġibba** at the €3.00 Special Services fare. Its route page displayed “Timetable not available!” when checked and no TWC-specific notice was found, so it is a possibility to watch for, not a plan — check service updates daily during 14–18 October. A 2023 Ta’ Qali festival precedent shows the pattern exists; its times are not transferable.
- **Official taxi tariff, with an honesty caveat:** Transport Malta publishes **maximum** meter rates directly — initial €3.50, then €2.50/km for the first kilometre stepping down to €0.85/km beyond 20 km, plus €25/hour waiting and €4.20/hour in traffic, regulated under S.L.499.59. But that page’s footer states **“Page last updated: 06/04/2022”**, so the maxima may have been revised. The worked caps on the site (roughly €12.40 at 5 km, €19.40 at 10 km, €25.90 at 15 km, €30.90 at 20 km) are **this project’s arithmetic at assumed distances, not quotes**, and the Qawra-to-venue road distance was never measured. Ride-hailing is dynamic and not bound by the tariff. Pre-book rather than rely on hailing: no taxi rank at an inland studio was verified.
- **Sports radio:** Malta-receivable evidence remains qualified. Radio in Malta labels “Sports Channel (Talk Sport)” on 6C (185.360 MHz); 2025 scanner-directory observations also point to 6C, while WorldDAB’s table lists a generic “Sports Channel” on 12A and the local operator directory lists no sports service. These are scan leads, not proof of current reception, identity or commentary. Official BBC episode records name seven UK Premier League commentaries for 17–18 October (including Bournemouth–Sunderland on 5 Sports Extra 2), but that UK schedule does not prove Malta carriage, BBC World Service output or stream access. NET FM’s grid and Rai’s weekend show are recurring schedules, not a trip-week named-match guarantee. A US HD Radio set cannot decode DAB+; the BBC advises using a DAB+ set purchased in Malta.
- **Kalshi, fixtures and eligibility:** EPL/Serie A category pages list trip-window fixtures that overlap the radio windows. Kalshi’s own help centre says trading from outside the United States is possible from many countries and that customers may use the platform while travelling abroad, subject to its Member Agreement and geographic requirements; third-party guidance conflicts, saying access is limited to US residents who pass KYC and warning of temporary geo-blocking while travelling. A market or fixture listing is still not proof of Malta radio commentary or of a market-to-broadcast link; confirm eligibility with Kalshi support.
- **Hotel environmental contribution:** the 2026 MTA guideline PDF lists €1.50 per night for eligible guests aged 18+, capped at €22.50 per person per visit, with separate receipt disclosure. The current MTA webpage separately refers to nights that “exceeded fifteen (15) nights”; the two official texts do not expressly reconcile that wording for short stays. Six nights × €1.50 is conditional arithmetic only, not a confirmed liability or planner default; ask MTA/AX ODYCY and use the invoice.
- **Expense planner:** all budget and actual fields start blank, including the seven optional daily templates. Two tools cover the itinerary hand-off: a documented “when your itinerary arrives” intake list, and a **paste-your-itinerary quick-fill** panel that previews which row each line would fill and writes only to still-blank budget fields — typed amounts are never overwritten, and unresolved lines are reported back instead of guessed. Enter euros on a consistent whole-trip/party basis and avoid counting the same expense in an aggregate and daily row. Inputs autosave only in this browser/device, are not sent to a server, can be exported as CSV (or downloaded blank as a template), and can be cleared. Verified bus/card prices are available as a transport benchmark; reference URLs are still not quotes.
- **Customs:** duty-free allowances into Malta (€430 by air/sea on goods, plus alcohol and tobacco limits, with a lower tobacco limit possible in some member states) come from the European Union’s official travellers’ page; the return leg uses U.S. Customs and Border Protection’s $800 returning-resident exemption (48 hours abroad, once every 31 days, 200 cigarettes / 100 cigars) and its declare-everything guidance. Check the Maltese customs authority for anything unusual.
- **Research-platform limits:** sampled TripAdvisor and a small number of Google Maps profile views informed parts of the food/activities research. Yelp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, and Reddit access was blocked or limited in the recorded checks; five 403 responses in one batch lacked reliable endpoint labels. Official X post text was extracted but its attached opening-day schedule image was not readable. Coverage is not comprehensive; see the dated source notes.

## Build, preview, and deployment

The site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with JSON data; it has no production build step or runtime dependency installation. Repository Pages settings publish the `main` branch root. Merging to `main` updates the public site through GitHub Pages. The `.nojekyll` file keeps the source/data tree static. GitHub Actions validates pull requests and pushes to `main`; the workflow is separate from Pages publishing.

For a local HTTP preview from the repository root:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080 --bind 0.0.0.0
# Open http://localhost:8080
```

An HTTP server is needed for browser `fetch()` and expense-planner `localStorage`. The deployed planner saves only in the current browser/device; export a CSV before switching devices or clearing browser data.

The offline validator checks JSON/schema/enums, source-log URL coverage, JavaScript syntax, topic-page/data wiring, and local paths/navigation. It is not an external URL crawler or a guarantee of current external facts.

## Browser and regression tests

Development tests use Node.js 22+, Python 3, and npm-distributed headless Chromium (Linux x64):

```bash
npm ci
npm test
```

The suite checks the nine active pages at 360/768/1440 px, responsive overflow, axe WCAG 2A/AA/2.1AA rules, data/render parity, navigation/deep links, filtering, expense persistence/CSV/reset, invalid amounts, denied/corrupt storage, and data-fetch failures. It does not test physical radio reception, external booking availability, personal eligibility, every browser/device, or exhaustive assistive-technology compatibility. Generated screenshots/reports and `node_modules` are ignored.
