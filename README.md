# Malta Trip Guide

A mobile-friendly, source-linked travel guide for the trip window **13–19 October 2026**, based at the user-supplied **AX ODYCY, Qawra Coast Road, Qawra SPB 1902** (Deluxe Sea-View room). The event location supplied by the traveler is **BLAST Arena Studios, Attard**.

**Published guide:** <https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/MALTASUPPLEMENTAL/>

The official Thunderpick page rechecked on 11 October lists the overall 2026 event window as 12–19 October (arrival 12th, media day 13th, Group Stage 14–16th, Playoffs 17–18th, departure 19th). The Thunderpick-issued release names BLAST Studios Malta; venue listings and BLAST’s attendee guide place BLAST Arena Studios at the MFCC South Gate National Stadium, Attard ATD 4000. None of these sources confirms this traveler’s spectator access, ticket, daily public door times, exact event entrance, or transport. Those details remain open.

This is a dated research and planning aid, not a booking or guarantee of future opening hours, fares, availability, event access, radio reception, or programme carriage.

## Key findings and limits

- **Event and venue:** the official Thunderpick page lists the 12–19 October event window and Malta as its location. The Thunderpick release names BLAST Studios Malta; BLAST Arena Studios is listed at MFCC South Gate National Stadium, Attard ATD 4000 (venue listings from Visit Malta, Eventworks and Liquipedia; a BLAST guide for another event gives MFCC/Ta’ Qali context only). The TWC entrance and gate are not separately confirmed. Public spectator access, ticket arrangements, door times, and the event-day entrance remain unconfirmed.
- **Event-day transit:** Route 186 is the leading public-bus corridor between Qawra and Ta’ Qali; Route 214 is an alternate to compare, and Airport Direct TD1 serves Il-Qawra stop Arznell (ID 950) hourly (05:42–00:35 from the airport; 05:27–23:27 toward the airport, checked 11 Oct). MPT’s static timetable (re-read 11 Oct) lists Qawra stop 952 every 30 minutes from 06:09 to 21:39 toward Rabat (alight at Stadium 6227, about 30 minutes later). The return leaves the opposite-side Stadium stop 2194, **last listed at 20:53**, and runs via St Paul’s Bay to Buġibba (last listed Buġibba Bay 1 arrival 21:27), not the Qawra seafront stops (950–955). Evening TWC sessions (community-wiki times: 14 Oct 18:00, 17 Oct 19:00 semifinal, 18 Oct 17:00 final) need Bolt, eCabs, Uber or Malta Taxi for the return. MPT also lists a 17 October 19:00–midnight diversion, a longer-term closure of Rabat-side stop Saqqajja (ID 4627), and Tuesday 13 October (09:30–17:30) construction detours on Attard Routes 54 and 106. Airport taxi desk fares (about €25 to Qawra) are third-party only.
- **Radio:** BBC World Service on Malta DAB+ (WorldDAB: block 6A, 181.936 MHz) schedules *Sportsworld* on 17 Oct (about 15:06–18:59 Malta time) and 18 Oct (about 16:06–19:59), overlapping the Premier League afternoon kickoffs (and explicitly bills its 11 Oct edition as including the English Premier League); the commentary match for 17–18 Oct is not yet announced. Rai Radio Uno (DAB+) carries Serie A in Italian on weekends. NET FM (FM 101 MHz) has recurring weekend sports shows. `radio.html` maps Premier League, Champions League and Serie A kickoffs (Malta time) to these options. Kalshi use from Malta depends on its Member Agreement and was not confirmed. Reception at the hotel and venue is untested; no radio carriage of the TWC was found. Unverified leads: a Friday 18:30 NET FM slot (‘Mhux Premier biss’, 16 Oct) and a WorldDAB ‘Sports Channel’ on Digi B 2 (12A). Premier League live audio is internet/app-based, and its availability in Malta was not confirmed; BBC Radio 5 Live Premier League listings are UK-based.
- **Hotel environmental contribution:** Malta Tourism Authority guidance checked 10–11 October states €1.50 per night per person aged 18+ from 1 July 2026, capped at €22.50 per eligible person per visit. Six chargeable nights would be €9 per eligible adult, conditionally; actual billable nights, guest ages, the ambiguous 15-night wording (OQ-25), and the hotel invoice determine the amount. The planner does not prefill a personal charge.
- **Expense planner:** all budget and actual amounts start blank. Enter amounts in euros on a consistent whole-trip/party basis. The planner stores inputs in this browser/device only, does not send them to a server, exports a CSV backup, and can clear saved entries. Reference URLs are not price quotes. (11 Oct: fixed a bug that left every row’s “Price status” blank.)
- **Research-platform limits:** sampled TripAdvisor and a small number of Google Maps profile views informed parts of the food/activities research. Direct Yelp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and X requests, and Reddit refreshes, were blocked or returned HTTP 403 in the relevant checks. Platform coverage is not comprehensive; see the dated notes in `docs/SOURCES.md` and the topic pages.
- **Open questions:** exact event access/times, saved date-specific transit route and return, walking route between TD1’s Il-Qawra Arznell (950) stop and AX ODYCY, 17 October diversion impact, trip-week radio schedule/carriage, current business hours/status conflicts, traveler-specific entry/insurance/device details, and the booking itinerary remain unresolved as noted in `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. (The 112 emergency number and TD1’s Il-Qawra stop Arznell 950 were verified from primary pages on 11 October.)

## Build, preview, and deployment

The site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with JSON data; no application build or dependency installation is required. Repository Pages settings checked on 9 October 2026 publish the `main` branch root to the public site. Merging changes to `main` updates the site through GitHub Pages’ existing branch-based publishing. The `.nojekyll` file keeps the source/data tree static. A GitHub Actions workflow validates pull requests and pushes to `main`; it does not replace the configured Pages publisher.

For a local HTTP preview from the repository root:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
# Open http://localhost:8080
```

An HTTP server is needed for browser `fetch()` and the expense planner’s `localStorage` behavior. The deployed planner saves only in the current browser/device; export a CSV before switching devices or clearing browser data.

The Pages workflow and local validator check the exact 13-field record schema and enums, HTTPS primary URLs and their source-log coverage, JavaScript syntax, topic-page/data wiring, and local links. External websites are not guaranteed to remain available; re-open primary sources before acting on date-sensitive information.
