Hotel & base — AX Odycy, Qawra

Everything on this site is now oriented to one address: AX Odycy, Qawra Coast Road, Qawra SPB 1902. That matters more than it sounds — Qawra is on the island's north-east coast, roughly 13 km from the airport, and it is not in the Sliema/St Julian's belt that most Malta guides assume. The single most useful fact on this whole site is that one bus line, route 186, runs from the seafront outside this hotel straight to the tournament venue at Ta' Qali, every 30 minutes, with no change of bus.

3Event days: hotel → BLAST Arena Studios, with times

Take route 186. It is direct, and it runs every 30 minutes all day. Route 186 goes Buġibba Bay 6 → Qawra seafront → Mosta → Ta' Qali Stadium → Ta' Qali Villagg → Rabat. The tournament venue is the Malta Fairs & Conventions Centre at Ta' Qali, beside the National Stadium. From Arznell at 06:08 the same bus is at the Ta' Qali stops by about 06:32 — roughly 24–30 minutes to the Ta’ Qali stops, no change, on the €25 weekly card.

Official Verified 9 Oct 2026 Departure times read directly from the operator's route 186 page. Thirty-two departures are published each way. A third-party bus atlas records the same route as roughly 32 minutes from Buġibba to the Ta' Qali Villagg stop.

The catch: there is no bus back after about 21:26 Two separate limits bite on a match night. First, the return direction does not come back along the Qawra seafront — it runs via St Paul's Bay (Mwiezeb, Imhasel, Toni, Gillieru) and the Buġibba seafront (Moll, Rizzi, Sajf, Gzejjer) to Buġibba Bay 1. So you get off at Buġibba Bay 1 and walk the promenade, or hop on any Buġibba→Qawra bus. Second, the last published return passing time at Buġibba Bay 1 is 21:26, and no night route (N11, N212, N48) serves Ta' Qali. Anything ending later than roughly 20:45 at the venue is a rideshare or taxi home. Budget for that before you go, because you will not be able to check a fare in advance — see Getting Around.

Buy the weekly card at Buġibba, not at the airport The operator lists sales offices at the airport (Welcomer's Hall and Baggage Reclaim), Valletta Terminal, Buġibba Terminal and Sliema Ferries, plus card machines and Agenda / 8TillLate / WH Smith outlets. Buġibba Terminal is the closest to this hotel, so if the airport queue is long you can buy the €25 Explore Adult 7-Day card after you arrive and pay cash or contactless for the single journeys in the meantime.

7Ask at check-in — five questions

  • Which of stops 950 / 951 / 952 / 953 is closest to this room? Note the code printed on the shelter.
  • What are the opening hours of Trattoria Riccardo, Cheeky Monkey and Espresso? None are published online.
  • Will you screen the Thunderpick finals? Unconfirmed either way — worth knowing before you plan around it.
  • Do you sell tallinja cards or know the nearest outlet? Buġibba Terminal is the operator's nearest listed sales office.
  • What does a taxi to Ta' Qali cost, and will you call one? No published fare exists for that pair; the front desk will have a working number.
