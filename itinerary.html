2Day-by-day skeleton

DayDatePlan (transport · food · activities)
1Tue 13 Oct
Arrival · media day		 Arrive MLA → TD1/TD5/214/X3 bus or taxi to AX ODYCY (~40–70 min / ~20–30 min; Getting Around § 0). Check in; buy the Explore Adult 7-Day card (€25) at the Buġibba Terminal sales office (5-min walk) or the airport. Afternoon: settle in — Malta National Aquarium (open to 20:00, €16.90 online) and the Qawra Point promenade; dinner at Venus (Buġibba) or a Tourist Street pastizzeria. Evening: UCL MD2 on the radio from 21:00 if you're flagging it.
2Wed 14 Oct
Event day 1 (group stage)		 Route 186 Qawra seafront → Ta' Qali (~25–30 min; go early for doors — session times TBC). If the session is late: Mosta Rotunda or Ta' Qali Crafts Village first. Lunch near the venue or pack from Qawra. Evening option: Gżira Utd v Mosta, 19:00, Paola (Malta FA ticketing) — or an early night.
3Thu 15 Oct
Sight day		 Mdina + Rabat morning (186 to Rabat, then 51/52/53; St Paul's Catacombs €6); St John's Co-Cathedral opens at 11:00 today — Valletta after lunch (bus 31/41/45 from Buġibba or 212/222 to Sliema + €3/€5 ferry). Alternative if calm weather: Ħaġar Qim & Mnajdra (€10) + Blue Grotto. Dinner: Gululu (St Julian's) or a Buġibba seafood pick (Food § 0). Europa League on the radio from 18:45 (carriage unverified).
4Fri 16 Oct
Event day 2 (group stage)		 Route 186 to the venue. Evening: Three Cities by ferry (Valletta → Cospicua, €3/€5) — Fort St Angelo (€10) / Inquisitor's Palace (€6) if open; the night sailing is the best cheap harbour view. Serie A MD7 opener Frosinone–Sassuolo 20:45 on Rai Radio 1.
5Sat 17 Oct
Event day 3 (playoffs) · race start		 Morning option: Rolex Middle Sea Race start from Upper Barrakka (Grand Harbour; time TBC) + Saluting Battery gun at 12:00. Then Route 186 to the venue for playoff day 1. BBC Sportsworld from 15:06 Malta (DAB+ 6A) across the 16:00 kick-offs; tune 6C "Sports Channel" at 13:25 for Everton–Chelsea (talkSPORT's slot). Note: Gozo Highspeed runs a restricted schedule today (race).
6Sun 18 Oct
Event day 4 — GRAND FINAL		 Route 186 to the venue for the Bo5 Grand Final + 3rd-place decider (session times TBC). BBC Sportsworld from 16:06 Malta (Forest–Arsenal 17:30). St John's Co-Cathedral is closed Sundays. If the final ends early: CDM Sundays at Café del Mar — FREE, ~10-min walk (from late afternoon to ~01:00) or Marsaxlokk Sunday fish market. Rai Radio 1: Fiorentina–Como 15:00, Milan–Atalanta 18:00, Juventus–Lazio 20:45.
7Mon 19 Oct
Departure day		 Check out; store bags if the flight is late. Late-flight option: Gozo (Highspeed from Buġibba €6.50, 30 min — Cittadella, Ġgantija, Ramla Bay) or Comino if flat calm; morning alternative: Hypogeum (if you booked a slot — €35) + Tarxien (€6), or Fort St Elmo (€10) / Grand Master's Palace (€12) en route south. To the airport: TD1/TD5/214/X3 (leave ~90 min before) or taxi (~€25–30). Last in-window fixtures (Spurs–Coventry 21:00; Monza–Cagliari 18:30; Parma–Torino 20:45) likely clash with your flight — don't plan around them.
