Known limitations & what's next

Limitations (honest audit)

  • Platform access: Google Maps, Yelp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X could not be queried directly (login-walled); their review signal reaches this site only via named aggregators (TripAdvisor, Wanderlog) with review counts shown. Reddit threads were used directly for gaming venues (flagged as community reports).
  • Point-in-time data: ratings, fares, multiplex line-ups and market counts move daily — every “Verified” tag records the check date (23 or 24 Sep 2026).
  • Known first-pass error rate: the 24 Sep re-verification withdrew eleven claims from the 23 Sep build (all single-source guidebook lines). Anything still tagged only “Verified 23 Sep 2026” has been read once, not twice.
  • Unresolved conflicts: BirguFest 2026 dates (four versions), the 2026/27 winter bus-fare switchover, and the 7-day card ↔ Airport Direct coverage contradiction remain open — all flagged on-page, none guessed.
  • Unpublished schedules: TWC match-day door times, talkSPORT's Malta feed for 17–19 Oct, BBC Sportsworld's commentary pick, Rai's October per-match assignments, and the Middle Sea Race start hour do not exist yet in public.
  • Automated link-checking not possible: the build sandbox has no outbound network from the shell, so outbound links were verified by reading each page through the research tool, not by an automated HTTP checker; the giveaway T&Cs pages were offline on 24 Sep (up briefly on 23 Sep).
  • Unpriced items: Comino/Blue Grotto boats, Saluting Battery, Fort St Angelo, Inquisitor's Palace, Ġgantija and the Heritage Malta multi-site pass are listed without prices rather than with guessed ones.
  • Not tax/legal advice: dollar bands are this site's arithmetic on cited rules; confirm filing treatment with a CPA and any eligibility question with Hotspawn in writing.
