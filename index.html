Executive Summary

Trip Overview

Dates: 13th October 2026 - 19th October 2026

Hotel: AX ODYCY Malta, Qawra Coast Road, Qawra SPB 1902 (Deluxe Sea View room)

Event Location: BLAST Arena Studios, Attard (MFCC South Gate National Stadium)

Key Findings & Verification

  • Transport: The most efficient way to travel between the AX ODYCY hotel and the BLAST Arena Studios in Attard is via ride-sharing apps (eCabs, Bolt, Uber), which takes ~25 minutes and costs €15-€25. Public bus (Route X3) is a cheaper (€2.50) but significantly slower alternative.
  • Food: Qawra offers excellent dining. The AX ODYCY hotel itself features top-rated options (Trattoria Riccardo, Minoa), while local cafes like Michele's Cafe are highly recommended nearby.
  • Everyday Life for US Travelers: Malta uses Type G (UK) plugs and 230V electricity. You will need an adapter. English is fluently spoken. Tipping is around 5-10%. Mobile connectivity is best solved with an eSIM (like Airalo) before arrival.
  • Radio Sports (Kalshi Markets): For tracking live English Premier League or other global sports, US travelers must use a DAB+ radio receiver. TalkSport broadcasts on DAB+ in Malta and carries live EPL matches, crucial for weekend trading on Kalshi. AM/FM receivers alone will not suffice for English sports commentary.
  • Activities: When not at the event, "The Gamers Lounge" in Msida offers PC gaming setups. The ancient city of Mdina is a highly recommended nearby landmark.
