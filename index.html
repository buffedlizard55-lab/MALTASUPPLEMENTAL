Executive summary · check these details before you travel
  • Event: The official TWC page lists the overall 12–19 October window (arrival 12th, media 13th, groups 14–16th, playoffs 17–18th, departure 19th) and gives Malta as the location; a Thunderpick-issued release names BLAST Studios Malta. That venue’s address — MFCC South Gate National Stadium, Millennium Stand, Attard ATD 4000 — was corroborated by three independent listings on 11 October 2026 (MTA directory, GamingMalta, Eventworks). Daily door times, ticket terms and the event-specific entrance are still unpublished.
  • Event-day transport: Route 186 is the leading public-bus corridor between the Qawra/Buġibba area and Ta’ Qali (Route 214 is an alternate to compare); its published evening service through the Mosta stretch ends around 21:53–21:55, and Malta Public Transport lists a Route 186 diversion weekly Friday and Saturday 19:00–00:00 starting 17 October (toward Buġibba, stops Gholja 2 to Targa skipped). Night routes run from Buġibba (N212) and Valletta (N13), but no night route serving Ta’ Qali was verified — plan a taxi/ride-hailing backup for late exits.
  • Sports radio: NET FM is listed at FM 101 MHz with weekend sports titles (Saturday “Hits & Score” 16:00, Sunday “Sports Box” 15:00). WorldDAB lists three Maltese DAB+ multiplexes — 6A (181.936 MHz), 6C (185.360 MHz) and 12A (223.936 MHz); BBC World Service and Rai Radio Uno appear on 6A, and 12A carries a service named “Sports Channel”. No reception test or fixture-specific commentary was confirmed, and a fixture being played is not evidence of radio carriage.
