MPT’s static timetable contains variants without reliable day-type context; no saved date-specific itinerary or safe walking route was checked. Its fare table lists €2.50 summer / €2.00 winter day-route singles, but the explicit winter period ends 13 June 2026 and the applicable 13–19 October 2026 fare is unconfirmed. Card prices/terms are separate comparators. Recheck MPT/tallinja, route disruptions and both directions after the organizer confirms the gate and finish time.
  • Sports radio: a specialist Radio in Malta directory labels a service “Sports Channel (Talk Sport)” on DAB+ 6C (185.360 MHz), and Wikipedia separately lists talkSPORT on 6C. WorldDAB lists a different generic “Sports Channel” on Digi B 2 / 12A (223.936 MHz), while the Malta DAB+ operator’s own directory lists no sports service. The block, service identity, programme, local reception and match carriage are unverified; scan and listen rather than assume. BBC World Service is listed on 6A; official Sportsworld episode data gives 17–18 October start times but describes general live sport, not a named match. NET FM’s recurring grid and Vibe FM’s domestic-league partnership are distinct evidence, not a trip-week named-match guarantee. A U.S. HD Radio set cannot decode DAB+; the BBC advises using a DAB+ radio purchased in Malta.
  • Everyday essentials: Malta uses Type G sockets at 230 V / 50 Hz, so U.S. plugs need an adapter and single-voltage high-wattage appliances also need a converter. Government of Malta and U.S. State Department sources confirm dialing 112 for emergencies. The 2026 MTA guideline PDF gives a €1.50-per-night eco-contribution for guests 18+ (cap €22.50), but the current MTA webpage separately says chargeable nights are those “that exceeded fifteen (15) nights.” The official texts are not clearly reconciled for a short stay; any six-night €9 amount is conditional arithmetic, not a confirmed charge. Customs guidance covers the EU’s €430 air/sea goods allowance and alcohol/tobacco limits, plus the U.S. $800 returning-resident exemption with its 48-hour/31-day conditions; check the everyday guide and official customs sources. Passport, insurance and medical guidance remains traveler-specific.
