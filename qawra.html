Your base: AX ODYCY Malta, Qawra

Qawra Coast Road, Qawra SPB 1902 · Deluxe sea view · 13–19 Oct 2026 (6 nights). This page covers the hotel and everything within walking distance — the promenade, the aquarium, the beach, the casino and the Buġibba hub — so you can plan the low-effort hours between matches. Every entry below is data-backed (data/qawra.json) and sourced.

Hotel AX ODYCY Malta (4★, formerly AX Seashells Resort) Venue BLAST Arena Studios, Ta' Qali — ~32 min by bus 186 Verified 9 Oct 2026

1The hotel

AX ODYCY is a large (≈565-room) 4-star seafront resort on Qawra's coast road — refurbished and rebranded from the old AX Seashells. The booked Deluxe Sea View is one of the mid-tier room types (Comfort → Deluxe → Junior Suite → suites with hot tubs). On-site there are multiple pools (including a rooftop), a small waterpark, a lido, and eight F&B outlets — so breakfast, a quick lunch and a post-match drink never require a bus.

  • Address: Qawra Coast Road, Qawra SPB 1902 · Phone: +356 2354 3000 · Licence H/0175
  • Check-in/out: 15:00 / 11:00 (third-party listing) — request a late checkout on the 19th if the flight is evening.
  • Eco-tax: €1.50 per adult per night (billed separately at check-out) — see Costs.
  • Location perk: the Qawra bus stops and the promenade are at the door; Buġibba terminus is a ~15 min walk; the Classic Car Museum is literally next door.

3The three evening loops

Loop A · zero effort

Hotel & promenade

Sunset walk to Qawra Point and back, dinner at Minoa/Luzzu/Cheeky Monkey, drinks at Medusa Sky Bar. 0–20 min on foot. The default after a full tournament day.

Loop B · 15 min walk

Buġibba Square evening

Walk the promenade to Buġibba for the big review-count restaurants (Venus, Victoria Gastropub, Ta' Pawla — see Food & Drink), gelato, and the buzz. Return by foot or a €5–8 Bolt.

Loop C · one bus

Sliema / St Julian's night

Bus 212 (or TD13) along the coast road to the Sliema/St Julian's strip — bigger restaurants, bayside bars, Paceville clubs. N212 brings you back after midnight (Fri/Sat certainty — check midweek). See Getting Around.

4Getting from here to the tournament

BLAST Arena Studios (Malta Fairs & Conventions Centre, Ta' Qali, Ħ'Attard) is ~10–12 km inland. The headline: bus 186 runs direct from the Qawra seafront stops to Ta' Qali (~32 min) — the only one-seat public-transport option. Full event-day transport plan, fares and the taxi fallback: Getting Around § 1.

Event-day rule of thumb Outbound by 186 (leave ~75–90 min before doors), return by Bolt/eCabs (≈€15–25 Estimate, split 2–3 ways) or the 186 until its ~22:30 last service — matches can end after the last bus.
