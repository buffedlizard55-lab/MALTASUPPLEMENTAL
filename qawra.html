Your base — Qawra, Buġibba & St Paul's Bay

The north-coast resort strip is one continuous neighbourhood: Qawra, Buġibba and St Paul's Bay run into each other along the coast, and your hotel is on the Qawra seafront. This page is everything within a short walk or one bus ride — food, cafés, beaches, the aquarium, events, and how to get to the venue from your front door. Full details live on the linked topic pages; this page mirrors data/activities.json, data/food.json and data/transport.json.

2Getting around from the hotel

  • Event days (venue): Route 186 (Buġibba–Qawra–Mosta–Ta' Qali–Rabat) from a Qawra seafront stop — ~25–30 min to Ta' Qali, every ~30 min. Return: 186 toward Buġibba (change/walk from Buġibba) or Route 214 (Buġibba–Qawra–Rabat–Ta' Qali–Balzan–Airport), which passes Qawra. Full plan + flags: Getting Around § 0.
  • The hub: Buġibba bus station (hub near the hotel) — 5-min walk; routes 186 (Ta' Qali/Rabat), 212 (Sliema/St Julian's), 221 (Mellieħa/Ċirkewwa), 222 (Sliema/Ċirkewwa), 223 (Golden Bay), 31/45/48 (Valletta/Mosta), X3/TD5 (airport). tallinja card sales office on site — buy the Explore Adult 7-Day card (€25) there on arrival.
  • Airport (Tue 13 Oct arrival / Mon 19 Oct departure): TD1 Airport Direct (Airport–Rabat–St Paul's Bay–Buġibba–Qawra–Mellieħa–Ċirkewwa) hourly €3.50; TD5 Airport Direct (Airport–Rabat–Buġibba) €3.00 in a limited midday window; Route 214 (€2.50/€2.00, 60–70 min); X3 airport express (Buġibba–Qawra–Mosta–Rabat–Ta' Qali–Attard–Airport) / X1 airport express (Airport–…–Qawra–San Paul's Bay–Mellieħa–Ċirkewwa) at standard fare; White taxi (airport fixed-fare kiosk) ~€25–28 fixed; Uber / Bolt / eCabs / Ryde ≈ €15–30 Estimate.
  • Late nights: Night buses (N1 / N11) serve the strip (mainly Fri/Sat); otherwise rideshare/taxi. Watch for TQ special event shuttles (Ta' Qali ↔ Qawra/Buġibba) — none announced for TWC as of 9 Oct 2026.

4Do near the hotel

  • Malta National Aquarium — Qawra Point, ~10-min walk, next to Café del Mar. Daily 10:00–20:00; adult €17.90 door / €16.90 online (official, 9 Oct 2026); 50+ tanks, ~300 species, 12-m main-tank tunnel; daily talks (sharks 10:30, main tank 13:00/16:00, octopus 14:30, reptiles 15:00, big fish 17:00).
  • Beaches near the hotel (Qawra Point & Buġibba, Salina Bay, Golden Bay/Għajn Tuffieħa) — rocky bays 5–10 min on foot; Salina Bay salt-pan walk; Golden Bay & Għajn Tuffieħa (best sand) ~20–30 min by bus (223/41/42). Free; sea ~23 °C Estimate.
  • St Paul's Islands & harbour boat trips from Qawra/Buġibba — island-hopper and Comino day-trip boats from the Buġibba/Qawra harbours Flag — operator prices unverified (listings: day trips ~€20–35, sunset ~€30–36); book on the day.
  • Xemxija & St Paul's Bay heritage shore — Roman-baths remains, Mistra Bay promenade; free.
  • Mosta Rotunda (Church of St Mary) — ~10 min by bus 186 (Mosta Rotunda stop); one of Europe's largest unsupported domes; small admission (price unverified).
  • Mdina & Rabat (Silent City + catacombs) — ~30–50 min (186 to Rabat + 51/52/53, or 31/45/48); pair with the Mġarr rabbit restaurants.
  • Oracle Casino Qawra — slots/table games/poker in town Flag — single guide source; confirm on the venue's listing.
  • 9 Ball pool & snooker hall — 366 Triq It-Turisti, Qawra; café on site; rainy-day hangout.
  • Gaming: no high-end venue in Qawra itself — Internet cafés near the hotel (Cyber Zone, A.A, 24/7 Internet Cafe)) cover basic PC access (Cyber Zone: Triq il-Port Ruman, Qawra; A.A: Triq Jean de la Valette, St Paul's Bay; 24/7: SPB 3115). For serious PC gaming: The Gamers Lounge (Msida), Esports Plaza Gaming Lounge (Sliema), Eden Esports Centre (St Julian's) — see Activities & Gaming (call ahead; prices/hours unpublished).

6Day-trip bases (with transport from Qawra)

  • Valletta — bus 31/41/45 from Buġibba (45–60 min) or 212/222 to Sliema + €3/€5 harbour ferry; the ferry lands below the city (Barrakka Lift up).
  • Gozo — Gozo Highspeed from the Buġibba jetty (€6.50 one way, 30 min; Flag — restricted schedule 14 & 17 Oct) or bus 221/222/X1 to Ċirkewwa + Gozo Channel (€4.65 return foot passenger). With a Mon 19 Oct departure, a Gozo day only fits a late flight — otherwise swap with another day.
  • Comino (Blue Lagoon) — boats from Buġibba/Qawra and Ċirkewwa; or bus to Ċirkewwa + third-party boats Flag — fares unverified.
  • South coast (Ħaġar Qim, Blue Grotto, Marsaxlokk) — bus toward the south, ~50–70 min; Dingli Cliffs en route.
  • Three Cities — harbour ferry from Valletta (or Sliema), €3/€5, every 30 min.

7Practical notes for the week

  • Weather in your window (13–19 Oct 2026): ~20–24 °C days, cooler evenings, sea ~23–24 °C; rain possible but not the norm — light layer + sunscreen Estimate — climate normals.
  • Shopping: supermarkets (e.g. on Triq It-Turisti), pharmacies and exchange bureaux on the strip; Sunday trading is limited — stock up Saturday for Sunday (St John's Co-Cathedral is closed Sundays too).
  • Safety: the strip is tourist-normal; the US State Department's Paceville warnings (drink-spiking, petty crime) apply mainly to Paceville — standard awareness suffices in Qawra/Buġibba. Emergency 112.
  • Money: euro; cards widely accepted (Amex less so); keep €20–40 cash for pastizzerias, market stalls and boatmen.
