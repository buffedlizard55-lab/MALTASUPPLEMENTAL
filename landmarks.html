Landmarks & sights worth your 8 days

Malta is small — the headline sights fit into three half-days around the match schedule, with room left for Gozo or Comino. The venue (BLAST Studios Malta, Ta' Qali) sits in the centre of the island, so nothing below is more than ~30–40 minutes away by bus or taxi. Prices and hours on this page were read from the operators' own sites on 24 Sep 2026 (Heritage Malta, St John's Co-Cathedral Foundation, the ferry companies); anything else is labelled as an estimate or a flag.

Corrections applied 24 Sep 2026 The earlier version of this page contained four errors that are now fixed: a non-existent temple (“Skara Baħri” — the real neighbours of Ta' Ħaġrat are the Skorba temples), “Cristal Lagoon” (it is the Blue Lagoon), a Caravaggio wrongly placed in Mdina (both Malta Caravaggios hang in St John's, Valletta), and the Malta Maritime Museum listed as a Day-8 option although Heritage Malta lists it as temporarily closed. Fort St Elmo houses the National War Museum only (not the National Archives).

1Valletta — the capital (UNESCO World Heritage)

SightWhat it isHours & price (official, 24 Sep 2026)
St John's Co-Cathedral The Knights' Baroque church on St John's Street; Caravaggio's Beheading of St John and St Jerome Writing hang in the Oratory. Allow ~1 h 45 (audio guide included). Dress code (shoulders/knees covered); no large bags; the museum wing is closed for refurbishment. Mon–Sat 09:00–16:45 (last admission 16:00); closed Sundays and public holidays. Adults €15, seniors/students €12, under-12 free; belfry climb +€5. Flag On Thu 15 Oct 2026 it opens late, at 11:00 (visitor notice dated 23 Sep 2026). Sun 18 Oct = closed.
Upper Barrakka Gardens & Saluting Battery Free public gardens with the classic Grand Harbour panorama over the Three Cities. The Saluting Battery below fires a ceremonial gun at 12:00 and 16:00 (Mon–Sat) — watch free from the terrace, or pay to enter the battery itself. The Barrakka Lift links the gardens with the harbour ferry landing. Gardens free, dawn–dusk. Battery entry ≈ €6 adult, Mon–Sat 10:00–16:30. Flag battery hours/price come from secondary listings — the operator page (Fondazzjoni Wirt Artna) returned 404 at check time.
Fort St Elmo — National War Museum Star fort at the tip of the peninsula (Great Siege 1565, WWII); the National War Museum is inside. 1.5–2 hours. Also where last-minute Hypogeum tickets are sold (see §4). Adult €10 (Heritage Malta). On “In Guardia” re-enactment Sundays, regular admission runs from 12:00 only. Flag whether Sun 18 Oct is an In Guardia date is not published yet.
Grand Master's Palace Seat of the Grand Masters and later the British governors; State Rooms and the Palace Armoury on St George's Square. Adult €12 (Heritage Malta). The Throne Room is currently closed; the State Rooms close on 26 & 28 Oct — after your window.
Republic Street · Merchant Street · Lower Barrakka The walkable UNESCO core. Coffee at Caffe Cordina (est. 1837); note the famous pastizzi bar Is-Serkin (Crystal Palace) is in Rabat, next to Mdina — not in Valletta. Free. Valletta is ~1 km end to end; the Sliema ferry lands below the city, the Barrakka Lift or a 10-minute climb takes you up.
Rolex Middle Sea Race start The 47th edition of the 606-nm offshore classic starts in Grand Harbour on Saturday 17 October 2026 — the fleet leaves between the Saluting Battery and Fort St Angelo, so Upper Barrakka is the free grandstand. Playoff Saturday at the event clashes, so treat this as a morning option. Free to watch. Flag the start time is not yet published (traditionally late morning) — check rmyc.org. Gozo Highspeed runs a restricted timetable on 14 & 17 Oct because of the race.

2Mdina & Rabat — the Silent City

  • Mdina — the walled medieval capital on a central ridge: lanes, St Paul's Cathedral, and bastion viewpoints over half the island. Vehicle access inside the walls is restricted to residents and permit holders, which is why it is quiet; go early or at dusk to beat the tour groups. No admission charge for the town itself.
  • Rabat (outside the gate) — St Paul's Catacombs, the largest Roman-era underground cemetery complex in Malta (Heritage Malta, adult €6), plus St Paul's Grotto beneath the parish church. Domvs Romana (Roman townhouse mosaics) sits between Mdina and Rabat.
  • Is-Serkin / Crystal Palace — Rabat's cult pastizzi bar, a two-minute walk from Mdina's gate; cash, counter service, pennies per pastizz.
  • Getting there: bus from Valletta (routes via the Rabat terminus) or ~15 minutes by taxi from Ta' Qali — Mdina is the closest major sight to the venue. Parking is scarce; don't drive in.

3The Three Cities (Birgu/Vittoriosa, Isla/Senglea, Bormla/Cospicua)

  • Across Grand Harbour from Valletta: the Knights' first home. Fort St Angelo (Birgu, Heritage Malta — adults €10; ~2 h visit; upper fort accessible via steps) and the Inquisitor's Palace (Birgu, Heritage Malta — adults €6): the two headline interiors; the waterfront marina and the Gardjola watchtower at the tip of Senglea are the photo stops. Official prices read directly from the Heritage Malta pages on 24 Sep 2026 (evening pass).
  • Flag Malta Maritime Museum (Birgu) is temporarily closed per Heritage Malta's site — do not plan around it.
  • Getting there: the Valletta–Three Cities ferry (Valletta Ferry Services, every 30 min, €3 single / €5 return by day) lands at Cospicua; the return sailing after dark is the best cheap harbour view in Malta. (Both ferry routes were back on the normal summer schedule at the 24 Sep evening check — the 23–24 Sep swell suspensions had ended; they recur in rough weather, so glance at the operator's homepage banner before setting out.)
  • BirguFest (Birgu by Candlelight) is normally held in October, but Flag the 2026 dates are still unconfirmed — the sources disagree even after a 24 Sep evening re-check: maltainfoguide (updated Jun 2026, names the Birgu Local Council as organiser) now asserts Fri 9 – Sat 10 Oct 2026, free entry, 18:00 onwards — i.e. before your window — while beautifulmalta says 16–17 Oct “preliminary” (inside), thetripverdict says 11–13 Oct with a €3 ticket covering Fort St Angelo + Maritime Museum + Inquisitor's Palace (matching Heritage Malta's 2019 festival pattern), and maltalingua lists “mid-October (TBA)”. Do not plan around it; confirm with the Birgu Local Council or watch the Heritage Malta What's-On page.

4Megalithic temples — older than Stonehenge and the pyramids

SiteWhat it isOfficial price / practical note
Ħaġar Qim & Mnajdra Archaeological Park (Qrendi)Two UNESCO temple complexes (c. 3600–3200 BC) on the southern cliffs, one ticket, one visitor centre; Mnajdra is a 500 m walk downhill from Ħaġar Qim. Late afternoon light is the classic time.Adult €10 (Heritage Malta). Bus from Valletta towards Qrendi/Ħaġar Qim; combine with the Blue Grotto (§5), 10 minutes away.
Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum (Paola)UNESCO underground necropolis (c. 4000–2500 BC); tours of 10 people, no photography, ~1 hour.Adult €35. Book online now — slots sell out weeks ahead. Heritage Malta releases a few last-minute tickets at €50 the day before (until 12:00 at Fort St Elmo or the Gozo Museum of Archaeology, then at the National Museum of Archaeology, Domvs Romana or the Hypogeum itself; max 4 per person). A Hypogeum ticket gives 50% off Tarxien.
Ħal Tarxien Prehistoric Complex (Tarxien)Four linked temples with the famous spiral reliefs and “fat lady” statue; under a protective canopy, so fine in wind or rain. 10 minutes' walk from the Hypogeum.Adult €6 (Heritage Malta).
Ta' Ħaġrat & Skorba (Mġarr)Two small UNESCO temple sites in the north-west village of Mġarr — low-key, 20–30 minutes each; the same village as the rabbit restaurants on the food page.Skorba & Ta' Ħaġrat combo ticket €6 adult (Heritage Malta store, read 24 Sep 2026 evening; range €4.50–6.00 by category). Limited opening days — Flag check the opening-hours page before going.

5Sea, cliffs & the other islands

  • Blue Grotto (Wied iż-Żurrieq) — sea caves lit turquoise from below; 20–25-minute boat rides from the tiny harbour, morning light is best, boats don't run in swell. Estimate Boat fare: €10 adult / €5 child per two 2026-dated guides (Rima Cruises, Outdoor Explorers — both Apr 2026); two other guides still list €8/€4, so treat €10 as current; no booking — buy at the on-site booth, cash (card not always taken), go before 10:00 for the light. Flag — guides, not an operator page; the €8/€10 conflict is shown
  • Dingli Cliffs — Malta's highest point (~250 m) and its emptiest coastline; free, bus-accessible, best at sunset. Nothing to pay for.
  • Mosta Rotunda — one of Europe's largest unsupported domes, 10 minutes from Ta' Qali; famous for the WWII bomb that pierced the dome without exploding. Small admission charge (not verified).
  • Marsaxlokk — the fishing village with painted luzzu boats; Sunday-morning fish market (18 Oct is a Sunday, but it's Grand-Final day); seafood lunch on the quay any day.
  • Popeye Village (Anchor Bay) — the 1980 film set turned theme park; a curiosity rather than a must.
  • Comino — the Blue Lagoon — the swimming/snorkelling cove between Malta and Gozo. In October the day-trip boats from Ċirkewwa, Buġibba and Sliema are still running but weather-dependent; the sea is still ~23 °C Estimate. Boat prices are third-party and not verified — Flag book on the day after checking the wind.
  • Gozo — the Cittadella above Victoria, Ġgantija, Ramla Bay, Dwejra's Inland Sea. Two ways over: Gozo Channel from Ċirkewwa (foot passenger €4.65 return, paid on the Gozo side, ~25 min, roughly every 45 min incl. nights) or Gozo Highspeed from Valletta (€7.50 one way, 45 min; Sliema €8.50; Buġibba €6.50). Flag Highspeed runs a restricted schedule on 14 & 17 Oct (Middle Sea Race) and Mġarr terminal works continue to 31 Oct; Sliema sailings were cancelled for swell on 23–24 Sep — always re-check the operator banner the night before.
  • Ta' Qali Crafts Village & National Park — next to the venue: glass-blowing and filigree workshops, the Malta Aviation Museum, and the park itself — a good filler between sessions.

Official Gozo Channel — passenger fares · Gozo Highspeed — fares & notices Verified 24 Sep 2026. Flag gozofastferry.com (the third fast-ferry brand) returned HTTP 500 at check time.

6Suggested 8-day sight plan (fits the 13–20 Oct window)
