Receiver quick check

  • DAB+ (recommended): use a DAB+ compatible receiver and run a full scan. Malta’s DAB+ directory lists BBC World Service, Rai Radio Uno and Radio Sportiva. WorldDAB lists BBC World Service on block 6A at 181.936 MHz; Radio Sportiva’s technical channel was not established. Directory entries are not reception tests, and an AM/FM-only radio will not receive DAB+.
  • FM: a regular FM receiver tuned to 101 MHz can try NET FM. Its Saturday and Sunday sports shows are recurring weekly listings, not a promise of live commentary, and the grid does not state its time zone.
  • AM / MW: no current sports broadcast was verified. Don’t rely on older references to Radju Malta on 999 MW; the cited history describes that as a past frequency.

EPL, soccer & live sports likely on Kalshi markets

  • English Premier League (EPL) & international soccer on DAB+ (BBC World Service, Block 6A · 181.936 MHz): BBC’s official Sportsworld upcoming broadcasts page (checked 11 October 2026) explicitly bills its Sunday 11 October edition (episode w1730c9208pxyf8) as “Live sport from around the world - including the English Premier League.” For the trip’s playoff weekend—Saturday 17 October (w1730c92ck0m2cw) and Sunday 18 October (w1730c92ck0q313)—the episodes are scheduled on “BBC World Service except News Internet” (3 hours 53 minutes each), though their synopses currently carry the generic description without naming specific EPL matches yet, and their displayed local start times depend on the viewer’s time zone. Separately, BBC Radio 5 Live’s Premier League page (m002h76y) is a UK national listing whose Malta reception is not established.
  • Serie A, European cups, F1 & MotoGP on DAB+ (Radio Sportiva): Digi B Network lists Radio Sportiva on Malta DAB+ (multiplex block/frequency not published). Its official palinsesto lists Diretta Sportiva (Sat/Sun 15:05–17:00) and states that live coverage extends up to 24:00 for Serie A, Serie B, Coppa Italia, European competitions, national-team matches, Formula 1, and MotoGP; dated 13–19 October live callouts were not yet posted on 11 October.
  • Maltese FM sports slots (NET FM · 101 MHz): NET FM’s recurring schedule lists Mhux Premier biss on Friday at 18:30 (16 Oct), Hits & Score on Saturday at 16:00 (17 Oct), and Sports Box on Sunday at 15:00 (18 Oct); the grid does not state whether these are studio roundups or live match commentary.
  • Kalshi market & fixture-list caveat: While EPL (see the 17–19 Oct Guardian fixture list below), Serie A, UEFA competitions, F1, MotoGP, and CS2 TWC matches are major sports categories commonly traded on prediction platforms such as Kalshi, neither a Kalshi contract listing nor a league fixture schedule is evidence of over-the-air AM/FM/DAB+ radio carriage or rights in Malta. Verify individual match commentary on the station pages on the day of broadcast.
