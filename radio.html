Live sport on the radio in Malta — and the Kalshi question

Everything below answers one question: which live sports broadcasts can I actually hear over the air (AM/FM/DAB+) in Malta during 13–20 October 2026, and which of those events carry Kalshi markets? Frequencies come from the DAB+ operator and receiver observations from 13 January 2026; the fixture schedule is the official leagues' own; anything that cannot be verified is flagged instead of assumed.

1What you need in your bag

Buy a DAB+/FM radio in Europe — a US radio will not do it US consumer radios use HD Radio (US/Iran system); Malta broadcasts DAB+ (Band III, 174–240 MHz) plus normal FM and some MW/AM. A US car radio or DAB-less portable will pick up FM only. Any European-market DAB+/FM receiver works — airport shops, electronics chains, and the DAB+ operator itself sells hardware. Newer EU-spec cars usually have DAB+ built in (US-spec cars generally don't).
  • FM works everywhere (car or portable) — that's how you'll get most Maltese stations.
  • DAB+ is where the international sports feeds live (Rai, talkSPORT, LBC, BBC World Service).
  • AM/MW: Radju Malta on 999 kHz plus strong foreign AM (Rai Radio 1 on 1062 kHz from Italy, Radio Tunis 630/963 kHz, Radio Algérie 531 kHz, Radio Libya 1053 kHz) — weak/intermittent depending on location and time of day.

5The Kalshi question

What Kalshi offers

Markets exist for the in-window sports

Kalshi's soccer section carries 663 markets across named competitions including the EPL, Champions League, La Liga, Bundesliga and MLS, with per-game YES/NO contracts plus league futures (title, top-4, match markets) — the EPL match markets are live week-to-week (e.g., Matchweek-4 match prices were published 12 Sep 2026).

The legal wrinkle

“Located in” — get it in writing

Answer: yes, trading from Malta is permitted under Kalshi's own rules. Kalshi's official help article “Can I trade on Kalshi from outside the United States?” (updated 20 Mar 2026) answers “Can I use Kalshi while traveling abroad?” with yes — provided access is consistent with the geographic requirements of the Member Agreement. That agreement (§VI, version 9.20.2026) bars trading while “domiciled in, organized in, or located in” a Restricted Jurisdiction; the list names 50+ countries — Malta is not among them. Flag Italy, France, the UK and Ireland ARE restricted — if your itinerary connects through Rome/Milan, Paris, London or Dublin, do not open or close positions during the layover. Kalshi keeps sole discretion to block access, and one third-party blog (tech-insider.org, Aug 2026, unverified) reports travellers sometimes seeing temporary geo-blocks — so screenshot the help article and expect a possible identity re-check. Funding while abroad: debit card, wire (min $1,000) or crypto; ACH/PayPal/Venmo are unavailable outside the US per the same article.

Practical notes

Set-up, not strategy

Keep your account details, 2FA and payment methods on a phone with a Malta SIM/eSIM. Contract availability is dynamic — a market for a specific game may not exist until close to kickoff, so don't pre-plan positions on assumption.
