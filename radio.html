Five-minute receiver test once you are in Malta
  1. Scan DAB+ blocks 6A, 6C and 12A (a pocket DAB+ radio or a phone with a DAB+ accessory; Malta broadcasts DAB+, not older DAB).
  2. On block 6C, find the service labelled “Sports Channel” and check whether it plays live match commentary or only studio talk.
  3. On block 6A, find BBC World Service and confirm Sportsworld on 17–18 October (about 15:06 and 16:06 Malta time).
  4. On FM, try NET FM 101 MHz for its weekend sports grid and Vibe FM 88.7 MHz for Malta Premier League coverage.
  5. Write down what each station actually carried at kickoff — that single note converts this page from leads into verified reception for the next session.
