Receiver quick check — what actually works coming from the US

  • Analog FM works. Maltese national FM stations sit inside the international FM broadcast band, which the ITU GE84 plan sets at 87.5–108 MHz for Region 1. Check the tuning range printed on any radio before relying on it; most phone FM chips are disabled in software.
  • Do not bring a US HD Radio set. The United States adopted HD Radio, a different digital standard; HD Radio and DAB/DAB+ receivers cannot decode each other’s signals.
  • DAB+ requires a compatible DAB+ receiver. Scan all three listed blocks — 6A (181.936 MHz), 6C (185.360 MHz) and 12A (223.936 MHz) — for BBC World Service and “Sports Channel.” The BBC specifically advises using a DAB+ radio purchased in Malta because UK DAB radios use a different codec. Directory entries are not an operator schedule, and no signal was tested at the hotel; confirm what the receiver actually plays.
  • FM to try first: NET FM at 101 MHz for its three weekend sports slots (Friday 18:30, Saturday 16:00, Sunday 15:00).
