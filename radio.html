Five-minute receiver check (do this once, then repeat at the venue)
  1. Scan block 6C (185.360 MHz) first for the sports pair: “Sports Channel” and Radio Sportiva. Then scan block 6A (181.936 MHz) for BBC World Service and Rai Radio Uno.
  2. You need a real DAB+ Band III receiver. The observed services run DAB+ stereo HE-AAC v2 at 32 kbit/s, so speech-grade audio is normal; a US HD Radio set will not receive DAB+.
  3. Compare FM while you are there: 101.0 NET FM, 88.7 Vibe FM, 93.7 Radju Malta, 92.7 ONE Radio, 103 RTK (regulator's nationwide list).
  4. Write down the service label, block, time, language and the fixture (if any) you actually heard. A programme name is not a match, and a directory entry is not a signal.
  5. Repeat inside the hotel room and near the venue — indoor reception is the untested half of every entry on this page.

What is still unverified, and what was corrected

Corrected in this pass. Earlier versions of this page told you to scan both 6C and 12A and to treat them as equal leads. That was wrong. A dated observation of Malta's multiplexes (13 January 2026) lists the “Sports Channel” service on 6C — service ID C02F at 32 kbit/s — and its 12A table shows only Smooth Platform music services, no sports service at all. The WorldDAB directory entry that placed a “Sports Channel” on 12A is therefore treated as unsupported for this trip and the record that carried it is marked unverified and superseded. Scan 6C; 12A is not where the sport is.

Block and identity. The label, service ID, bitrate and block are established by observation, and an independent Malta directory agrees on the ID. The content identity stays third-party: a 2023 enthusiast field report of the same DIGI B 3 ensemble labelled the service “(Talk Sport)” and pointed at talksport.com, and the operator's own consumer channel directory renders no sports tile. Nothing checked proves what that service carries on 17–18 October 2026.

Premier League specifically. The UK commentaries you may be used to are on BBC Radio 5 Live, BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra and talkSPORT. BBC 5 Live and 5 Sports Extra do not appear on Malta's observed multiplexes, and BBC World Service — which is carried, on 6A — runs general live sport in Sportsworld, not match commentary. So do not expect English-language EPL commentary over a Maltese receiver; treat any EPL audio as a bonus if you find it and a bonus only.

Serie A reads better. Rai Radio 1's Tutto il calcio minuto per minuto is a Saturday-and-Sunday programme with live match commentary on Serie A and Serie B, and Rai RadioUno is on the observed 6A service list. Both of its air days fall inside the trip. Radio Sportiva, a 24-hour Italian sports station, is separately confirmed on 6C. Neither has published which match it will cover on 17 or 18 October.

Still unverified, and only you can close it: printed FM/DAB+ schedules for the exact trip dates; which matches the Italian services select; any English-language football commentary on a Maltese frequency; the “Sports Channel” relay's actual audio; and indoor reception at AX ODYCY and at the Ta' Qali venue.
