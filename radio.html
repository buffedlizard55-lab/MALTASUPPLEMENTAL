Receiver quick check

  • DAB+ (recommended): use a DAB+ compatible receiver and run a full scan. Malta’s DAB+ directory lists BBC World Service, Rai Radio Uno and Radio Sportiva. WorldDAB lists BBC World Service on block 6A at 181.936 MHz; Radio Sportiva’s technical channel was not established. Directory entries are not reception tests, and an AM/FM-only radio will not receive DAB+.
  • FM: a regular FM receiver tuned to 101 MHz can try NET FM. Its Saturday and Sunday sports shows are recurring weekly listings, not a promise of live commentary, and the grid does not state its time zone.
  • AM / MW: no current sports broadcast was verified. Don’t rely on older references to Radju Malta on 999 MW; the cited history describes that as a past frequency.
