What is confirmed—and what is not (re-checked 11 October 2026)

Start with NET FM on FM 101 MHz. For DAB+, WorldDAB lists three Maltese multiplexes — Digi B Network on 6A (181.936 MHz), Digi B 3 on 6C (185.360 MHz) and Digi B 2 on 12A (223.936 MHz). BBC World Service and Rai Radio Uno appear on 6A, and 12A carries a service simply named Sports Channel. Rai Radio Uno publishes weekend live Italian football coverage; the brand behind “Sports Channel” is not established by the directory.

Receiver quick check

  • FM: a regular FM receiver that tunes to 101 MHz can try NET FM. Its Saturday and Sunday sports shows are recurring weekly listings, not a promise of live match commentary; the grid does not explicitly state its time zone.
  • DAB+: use a DAB+ compatible receiver and scan all three multiplexes (6A, 6C, 12A). BBC World Service and Rai Radio Uno are listed on 6A; a “Sports Channel” is listed on 12A. Radio Sportiva appears in Digi B’s channel directory but is not named in WorldDAB’s current 6A/6C/12A service lists. Directory entries are not reception tests, and an AM/FM-only radio will not receive DAB+.
  • AM / MW: no current sports broadcast was verified. Do not rely on older references to Radju Malta on 999 MW; the cited history describes that as a past frequency.

Unverified leads and event limits

WorldDAB does list a regular DAB+ service named “Sports Channel” on Digi B 2 (block 12A), which is recorded above — but the directory does not say who provides it. A third-party Malta directory separately claims a “Sports Channel (Talk Sport)” on 6C; that brand claim is not confirmed by the multiplex operator, so TalkSPORT is not counted as verified receivable radio. Practical approach: scan by service name on 12A and 6C and identify the broadcaster by listening. Third-party directory: Radio in Malta’s DAB+ 6C list.
