What is confirmed—and what is not

Start with NET FM on FM 101 MHz. For DAB+, Digi B Network’s channel directory lists Radio Sportiva and BBC World Service; use a DAB+ receiver and scan by station name. The directory does not give a current channel block/frequency, and reception has not been tested at the hotel or event venue.

No current AM/MW sports programme was verified. A fixture or event date is not evidence of broadcast carriage: no radio commentary has been confirmed for the Thunderpick event, Gżira United–Mosta, or the Rolex Middle Sea Race.

Receiver quick check

  • FM: a regular FM receiver that tunes to 101 MHz can try NET FM. Its Saturday and Sunday sports shows are recurring weekly listings, not a promise of live match commentary.
  • DAB+: use a DAB+ compatible receiver and scan for Radio Sportiva or BBC World Service. A standard AM/FM-only radio will not receive DAB+.
  • AM / MW: no current sports broadcast was verified. Do not rely on older references to Radju Malta on 999 MW; the cited history describes that as a past frequency.
