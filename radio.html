1What you need in your bag

Buy a DAB+/FM radio in Europe — a US radio will not do it US consumer radios use HD Radio (US/Iran system); Malta broadcasts DAB+ (Band III, 174–240 MHz) plus normal FM and some MW/AM. A US car radio or DAB-less portable will pick up FM only. Any European-market DAB+/FM receiver works — airport shops, electronics chains, and the DAB+ operator itself sells hardware. Newer EU-spec cars usually have DAB+ built in (US-spec cars generally don't).
  • FM works everywhere (car or portable) — that's how you'll get most Maltese stations.
  • DAB+ is where the international sports feeds live (Rai, talkSPORT, LBC, BBC World Service).
  • AM/MW: only Radju Malta on 999 kHz is a verified domestic MW service. Correction An earlier version listed Rai Radio 1 on 1062 kHz — Rai closed its entire medium-wave network on 11 Sep 2022, so there is no Italian sport on AM any more; other North-African MW signals are not verified and are not relied on here. Treat AM as irrelevant to this plan.

5The Kalshi question

What Kalshi offers

Markets exist for the in-window sports

Kalshi's soccer section carries 663 markets across named competitions including the EPL, Champions League, La Liga, Bundesliga and MLS, with per-game YES/NO contracts plus league futures (title, top-4, match markets) — the EPL match markets are live week-to-week (e.g., Matchweek-4 match prices were published 12 Sep 2026).

The legal wrinkle

“Located in” — get it in writing

Answer: yes, trading from Malta is permitted under Kalshi's own rules. Kalshi's official help article “Can I trade on Kalshi from outside the United States?” (updated 20 Mar 2026) answers “Can I use Kalshi while traveling abroad?” with yes — provided access is consistent with the geographic requirements of the Member Agreement. That agreement (§VI, version 9.20.2026) bars trading while “domiciled in, organized in, or located in” a Restricted Jurisdiction; the list names 50+ countries — Malta is not among them. Flag Italy, France, the UK, Ireland, Switzerland, Belgium, Portugal, Poland and Hungary ARE on the restricted list (among ~50 jurisdictions) — if your itinerary connects through Rome/Milan, Paris, London, Dublin, Zurich or Brussels, do not open or close positions during the layover. Germany, the Netherlands, Denmark and Austria (Frankfurt, Munich, Amsterdam, Copenhagen, Vienna hubs) are not on the list (full §VI list re-read 24 Sep 2026: 55 named jurisdictions plus US-sanctioned territories; neither Malta nor those hubs appear). Re-read the agreement before you fly — it is versioned (9.20.2026 at check time) and Kalshi may amend it unilaterally. Kalshi keeps sole discretion to block access, and one third-party blog (tech-insider.org, Aug 2026, unverified) reports travellers sometimes seeing temporary geo-blocks — so screenshot the help article and expect a possible identity re-check. Funding while abroad: debit card, wire (min $1,000) or crypto; ACH/PayPal/Venmo are unavailable outside the US per the same article.

Practical notes

Set-up, not strategy

Keep your account details, 2FA and payment methods on a phone with a Malta SIM/eSIM. Contract availability is dynamic — a market for a specific game may not exist until close to kickoff, so don't pre-plan positions on assumption.
