Research limitations

The site prioritizes official operators, organizers, venues, and government sources for schedules, entry guidance, prices, and transport; specialist directories are identified separately where relevant. Review and social data are labelled as platform snapshots, not guarantees or universal rankings. Direct Yelp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Reddit requests were blocked or limited; official X post text was available but its attached schedule was not; Google Maps views were limited and did not expose a comprehensive set of review text. These gaps are recorded, not inferred around.

Radio schedules and a DAB+ directory listing do not prove that a specific match or event is carried or receivable at the hotel/venue. Transit-route candidates do not replace a date/time-specific journey planner query.

Before acting on a listing

  • Recheck a venue’s official page or contact the operator for current hours, reservation status, pricing, and access.
  • Run Malta Public Transport’s planner for the actual event-day time and destination entrance; check service alerts, especially the 17 October Route 186 notice.
  • Reserve a free in-person ticket and carry photo ID — attendance is listed as ages 18+. Confirm the €0 price at checkout, since the ticketing page rendered no price table, and re-check the Saturday 17 / Sunday 18 door times, which were not published. Ask about the attendee gate, entrance and parking, none of which appeared in any source read.
  • Pre-book a taxi or ride-hailing return for every event night. The venue is inland, there is no verified night bus to Ta' Qali or Attard, and no taxi rank at the venue was confirmed. Check MPT's service updates daily for a TQ1 special event service through Qawra.
  • Confirm the current single bus fare when you pay. The published €2.00 winter window ends 13 June 2026, so it does not apply to this trip, and the €2.50 summer row is undated.
  • WorldDAB lists BBC World Service on Digi B Network block 6A (181.936 MHz); Radio Sportiva’s technical channel remains unverified. Scan with a DAB+ receiver locally—directory data is not a reception test—and confirm schedules close to airtime.
  • Use the expense planner’s blank fields for confirmed amounts and download a private CSV backup before changing devices.
