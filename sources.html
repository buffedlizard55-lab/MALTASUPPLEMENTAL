Research limitations

The site prioritizes official operators, organizers, venues, and government sources for schedules, entry guidance, prices, and transport. Review and social data are labelled as platform snapshots, not guarantees or universal rankings. Direct Yelp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, and Reddit requests were blocked or limited in the relevant checks; Google Maps views were limited and did not expose a comprehensive set of review text. These gaps are recorded, not inferred around.

Radio schedules and a DAB+ directory listing do not prove that a specific match or event is carried or receivable at the hotel/venue. Transit-route candidates do not replace a date/time-specific journey planner query.

Before acting on a listing

  • Recheck a venue’s official page or contact the operator for current hours, reservation status, pricing, and access.
  • Run Malta Public Transport’s planner for the actual event-day time and destination entrance; check service alerts, especially the 17 October Route 186 notice.
  • Ask the organizer about public entry, ticket entitlements, doors, and finish times. Tournament dates alone do not establish spectator access.
  • Rescan/check a DAB+ receiver locally; the directory does not publish an exact block/frequency or guarantee reception. Confirm station schedules close to airtime.
  • Use the expense planner’s blank fields for confirmed amounts and download a private CSV backup before changing devices.
