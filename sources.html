How to read the topic records

  • Source type: official means a venue, operator, organizer, broadcaster or public authority; map, review and social distinguish other evidence rather than elevating it to official confirmation.
  • Confidence: verified applies to the specific claim supported by the linked evidence; partially verified signals that a detail or condition remains open; unverified marks a lead that should not be treated as a confirmed service, price or schedule.
  • Unknown values: missing hours, addresses, costs and schedules are left blank or explicitly identified as not published/not verified. A planner blank is not a zero-cost claim.
  • Conflicts and access limits: differing listings, unavailable platforms, incomplete schedules, and untested reception are disclosed in the relevant records and registers. They are not resolved by guessing or averaging.
  • Point-in-time facts: prices, hours, review counts, weather, event status, transport and entry rules can change. Confirm with the original source close to the date of use.
