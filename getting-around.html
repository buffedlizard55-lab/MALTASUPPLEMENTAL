Published fare table and card prices (not a trip-date fare quote)

MPT’s fare table lists a day-route single at €2.50 (summer) or €2.00 (winter), valid two hours with free transfers; night routes and Tallinja Direct €3.00; TD1 airport express €3.50; €19 for a shareable 12-journey card (Night Routes and Airport Direct count 2 journeys per trip); €25 Explore Adult 7-day; €7 Explore Child 7-day; and Explore Flex €21 (4 days) / €27 (7 days), with an advertised €6 add-on for two Airport Direct trips. A €50 fine applies without a valid ticket. Cards are listed at Buġibba Terminal, the airport, Valletta Terminal and Sliema Ferries. The operator’s own page still prints only the 19 Oct 2025 – 13 Jun 2026 winter window, but two independent third-party 2026 fare guides place the season boundary at 18/19 October — matching that window’s 19-October winter start — so the likely split is €2.50 on 13–18 October and €2.00 on 19 October. That split is a partially verified inference, not an operator confirmation: check the MPT app/page before relying on it.

Compare products only after the ride plan is known: the €27.00 Explore Flex 7-day offer grid includes Day Routes, Night Routes, Airport Direct and Special Services. Explore Adult 7-day is €25.00 and its fare-page paragraph excludes tallinja Direct routes, while the same page's “Staying for a week” summary says a 7-day card includes Airport Direct — the page contradicts itself on that point, so a traveller who needs the airport coach should ask MPT or buy the €27 Explore Flex, whose own paragraph describes the Airport Direct add-on path. Clarify with MPT before purchase. A return airport trip in TD1 singles is €7.00 at the listed €3.50 rate, but whether you need TD1 or any card is traveler-specific. No pass or ride count is assumed.

Which mode fits?

Planning judgments below, not measured journey times or quotes. Sources and published prices are in the transport records.

Bus · value for flexible days

Published single/pass prices make budgeting possible. Choose when the live route, safe walks and return fit. Waiting, interchanges and final venue access remain unmeasured.

Ride-hailing / taxi · convenience

Door-to-door candidate for luggage or late finishes. Live quote required; compare whole-party cost and pickup instructions. Vehicle supply and pickup permission for event times are not confirmed.

Rental car · only if justified

Compare rental, insurance/excess, fuel and parking as a whole. Left-side driving and narrow/congested roads add effort; event parking is not verified.

Walk / ferry · local exploration

Ask locally about safe Qawra walks; no hotel-to-Attard walking route is recommended. Ferries serve sightseeing corridors, not a direct Ta’ Qali commute; weather and connections matter.
