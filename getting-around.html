Venue: the official TWC page gives Malta only, but the venue it names — BLAST Arena Studios Malta — is published by three independent listings as MFCC South Gate National Stadium, Millennium Stand, Attard ATD 4000 (Malta Tourism Authority directory, GamingMalta, Eventworks). The organizer has still not published the event-specific gate, doors or ticket terms, so confirm entry instructions before travelling.

Event-day bus: Route 186 is the direct Qawra/Buġibba ↔ Ta’ Qali corridor line, but its published evening service ends around 21:53–21:55 on the Mosta stretch, and on Saturday 17 October from 19:00 to midnight Malta Public Transport lists a diversion that skips stops from Gholja 2 through Targa toward Buġibba. Night buses exist at Buġibba (N212) and Valletta (N13); no night route serving Ta’ Qali was verified, so plan a taxi/ride-hailing backup for a late exit.

Fares, stop access and diversions can change; recheck the live timetable and service notices on the day.
