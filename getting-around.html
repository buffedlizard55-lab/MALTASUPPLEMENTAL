Getting around Malta — convenience, price, value, feasibility

Malta is 27 km long. The honest answer: you do not need a car. Buses (the integrated tallinja system) are the value play, the airport taxi kiosk is the convenience play, and Uber/Bolt/eCabs are the bridge between them. Everything below is priced from the official operator and current guides.

2The official bus system (tallinja) — what you'll actually use

  • Pay cash or tap a contactless bank card on the bus; the integrated card/app is tallinja (download before you fly — ticket sales at the airport, Valletta Terminal, Buġibba, Sliema Ferries, Agenda/8TillLate/WH Smith retail, or the app).
  • Single journey (Day Routes): winter €2.00 / summer €2.50, valid 2 hours including one transfer. Flag The operator's page states the winter fare window as 19 Oct 2025 → 13 Jun 2026; the 2026/27 window is not yet published but the pattern has switched on 19 October — mid-trip (your Oct 13–20 straddles it). Check the operator page a week before departure; at worst you overpay €0.50 per ride, or the card covers you either way.
  • Cards (all valid on Malta + Gozo buses):
    • Explore Adult 7-Day — €25: unlimited Day Routes, Night Routes, selected Special Services (excludes tallinja Direct). For your 7 nights this is the money card.
    • 12 Single Day Journeys — €19, shareable, 6-month validity, free transfers within 2 h; Night Routes/Airport Direct count 2 of the 12 journeys per trip.
    • Explore Flex 4-day — from €21 / 7-day — €27: unlimited bus + a pick of a Gozo return, harbour cruise, or both; can be topped up to pay the Valletta↔Sliema/Three Cities ferry.
    • Explore Child 7-day — €7 (ages 4–10; under 4 free).
  • Airport Direct: €3.00 per passenger (€3.50 on the TD1 route). For your base: TD3 serves St Julian's, Sliema, Gżira and Msida every 30 minutes (TD2 covers St Julian's/Pembroke; TD4 Valletta + Gozo Fast Ferry) — buses have luggage space, USB charging and free WiFi, no booking needed. Flag The two official operator pages contradict each other on card coverage: the Airport Direct page's FAQ says “If you have an Unlimited Travel Card, Airport Direct is included at no extra cost”, while the Explore Adult 7-Day card detail says it excludes tallinja Direct routes (the TD routes are Airport Direct). Confirm at the airport kiosk before relying on it; buying the hops separately is €3 each direction either way.
  • Night routes & special services: €3.00; tallinja Direct express routes €3.00 (TD1 €3.50).
Recommendation for your trip Buy the Explore Adult 7-Day (€25) at the airport on arrival, add the Airport Direct hops separately (€3 each direction), and keep a card/tap for the Valletta ferry or Vaporetto. That's a week of island travel for under ~€35 — the best value-to-convenience ratio on the island. Save the €20–27 airport taxi for the day you land with heavy bags if you'd rather not queue for the bus.

3Ride-hailing — the fine print

  • Uber operates in Malta (launched summer 2022). At the airport you can request UberX (not the Uber Taxi product), with a dedicated pickup zone — check the app for the exact meeting point. No advance bookings on Uber.
  • Bolt and eCabs both operate too and support advance bookings — useful for a fixed 07:00 to Ta' Qali on a Grand-Final morning.
  • Prices are app-determined (surge applies in peak demand). The airport→Sliema band is ~€20–35; it's a per-car price, so it gets great value with 2–4 people splitting.
  • Coverage is densest in the Sliema/St Julian's/Paceville belt and thins to rural central/southern routes — for Mdina or the Ħaġar Qim sunset, the bus (or a reserved Bolt) beats hoping for an app ping.

4Taxis — the regulated option

  • Official white taxis are regulated; at the airport there's a 24/7 fixed-fare kiosk in the arrivals hall — you pay there, get a receipt, and hand it to the waiting driver. Flag the airport's old fixed-fare table page has moved: the airport's transport page now directs passengers to the official licensed operator Malta Taxi (fare calculator/app). Corroborating traveler reports and transfer guides put the fixed Sliema fare at ~€20 plus a small admin fee (~€23–27 all-in). Confirm your exact fare at the kiosk before handing over the receipt.
  • Away from the airport, taxis run on meters — agree/expect metering; ask for a receipt. There's no luggage surcharge on airport kiosk fares (bags must fit the boot).
  • Pre-booked private transfers (Welcome Pickups etc.) run ~€25–40 to Sliema — a comfort upgrade, not a savings play.

6Verdict — best way to get around

For convenience, price, value and feasibility combined
  • Value champion: public bus + Explore 7-Day card (€25). Unbeatable per-km; covers every sight and the venue.
  • Convenience champion: white-taxi kiosk at the airport for the two runs you care most about (landing and leaving) with luggage; fixed, published, 24/7.
  • Feasibility bridge: Bolt/eCabs for reserved early-morning event runs; Uber for on-demand in the belt.
  • Car: skip it unless you commit to Gozo + Comino + south coast in 48 h. Left-hand driving, tight parking, and an island that's ~30 min corner-to-corner make it a hassle most weeks.
Realistic all-week transport spend for one person: ~€35–60 (card + airport hops + 2–3 rideshare runs).
