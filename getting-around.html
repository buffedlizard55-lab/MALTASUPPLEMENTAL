Mode comparison: convenience, price, value & feasibility

  • Public buses (Malta Public Transport): Price & value: Lowest cost (€2.00 winter / €2.50 summer 2-hour day single, with October 2026 seasonal cutoff unconfirmed; €3.50 Airport Direct TD1; €19 shareable 12-journey card; €25 7-day Explore Adult; €27 7-day Explore Flex). Convenience & feasibility: Strong daytime feasibility for Qawra ↔ Ta’ Qali via Route 186 (Qawra stop 952 at 06:09–21:39 to Stadium stop 6227; compare Route 214) and hourly airport service via TD1 (serving Il-Qawra stop Arznell 950 at 05:42–00:35 outbound and 05:27–23:27 return), but lower late-evening convenience because Route 186’s return runs via St Paul’s Bay/Buġibba (last departure 20:53 from Stadium 2194; last arrival Buġibba Bay 1 at 21:27) rather than the Qawra seafront and is diverted on Saturday 17 October from 19:00 to ~00:00.
  • Ride-hailing & taxis (Bolt, eCabs, Uber, Malta Taxi): Price & value: Higher per-trip cost (live app/meter quote required; U.S. State Department notes white taxis are metered and expensive while app rideshares are widely available), best value when splitting rides or returning late. Convenience & feasibility: Highest door-to-door convenience and the most reliable late-night backup after TWC Playoffs or with airport luggage.
  • Ferries (Valletta Ferry Services, Gozo Channel, Gozo Highspeed): Price & value: High scenic value at published fares (€3.00/€5.00 Valletta harbour day single/return; €4.65 Gozo Channel standard passenger; €6.50–€8.50 Gozo Highspeed adult one-way). Convenience & feasibility: Practical only for harbour crossings (Sliema ↔ Valletta ↔ Three Cities) and Gozo day trips; weather-sensitive, with 14 & 17 October Gozo Highspeed race restrictions and no coverage of inland Attard/Ta’ Qali.
  • Rental car (MLA Park East): Price & value: Variable daily rental, insurance, fuel, and parking costs (live quote required). Convenience & feasibility: Useful only if prioritizing remote coastal stops; lower feasibility/value for a Qawra-to-Ta’ Qali event trip due to left-hand driving, narrow congested roads, and parking friction.
