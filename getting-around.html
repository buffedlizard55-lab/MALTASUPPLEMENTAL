Mode comparison · convenience, price, value & feasibility

Summary of the sourced records in data/transport.json across your three main travel needs (airport transfer, event-day commute, and island sightseeing):

Best value · scheduled corridors

Public buses (Route 186, Route 214 & Airport Direct TD1)

Price & value: Lowest published fares—TD1 is €3.50 without an eligible unlimited card; regular day routes list €2 winter / €2.50 summer cash/contactless (2-hour transfer window; exact Oct 2026 seasonal cutoff unconfirmed), €19 shareable 12-journey card, or €25 Explore Adult 7-day card.

Feasibility & convenience: Route 186 links Qawra stop 952 (listed 06:10–21:38, 30-min frequency) and Ta’ Qali Stadium stop 2194; Route 214 links Qawra stop 952 and Ta’ Qali stop Qali 2 (2542) as well as the airport. However, the standard Route 186 return from Rabat/Ta’ Qali runs via St Paul’s Bay/Buġibba rather than the Qawra seafront (last listed arrival at Buġibba Bay 1 is 21:26), and Saturday 17 October has a 19:00–00:00 detour toward Buġibba omitting stops Gholja 2 through Targa.

Highest convenience · late-return backup

Ride-hailing & taxis (Bolt, eCabs & Malta Taxi)

Convenience & feasibility: Best door-to-door option when carrying luggage from the airport, when event sessions finish after 21:00, or during the Saturday 17 October evening Route 186 diversion.

Price & value: Higher cost than public buses; fares and car supply are dynamic and must be checked live in the Bolt or eCabs app or with Malta Taxi (+356 2182 3017 advertised 24/7).

Optional · multi-stop rural trips

Rental car (Airport Park East Level 1)

Convenience & feasibility: Pre-booked rental desks are at Park East Level 1 (about a 3-minute walk from the terminal). Useful for flexible rural sightseeing, but not needed to reach Qawra or Ta’ Qali.

Price & limits: Requires live quotes for rental, insurance excess, fuel, and parking; Malta drives on the left and the U.S. State Department notes narrow, congested roads.

Harbour & island crossings

Ferries (Valletta Ferry, Gozo Channel & Gozo Highspeed)

Convenience & feasibility: Fixed-corridor water links rather than general island transit. Valletta Ferry Services links Valletta with Sliema and the Three Cities; Gozo Channel (Ċirkewwa ↔ Mġarr) and Gozo Highspeed (Valletta/Sliema/Buġibba ↔ Gozo) serve Gozo excursions, with Gozo Highspeed restrictions noted on 14 and 17 October during the Rolex Middle Sea Race.

Price: Published adult fares are €3/€5 day single/return (€3.50/€5.20 night) on Valletta ferries, €4.65 standard passenger tariff on Gozo Channel, and €6.50–€8.50 one-way on Gozo Highspeed.
