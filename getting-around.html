Getting around Malta — from Qawra, on event days and off

Malta is 27 km long and you do not need a car. From AX ODYCY the answers are simple: bus 186 goes direct to the venue, TD1 covers the airport, and Bolt/eCabs fill every gap. Everything below is priced from the official operator (publictransport.com.mt, checked 9 Oct 2026) and current timetable sources, with estimates labelled.

1Event days: AX ODYCY ↔ BLAST Arena Studios (Ta' Qali)

The answer, up front Take bus 186 from the Qawra seafront / Buġibba terminus toward Rabat and alight at Ta' Qali Villagg or Ta' Qali Stadium — the Malta Fairs & Conventions Centre (BLAST Arena Studios) is right there. Journey ≈ 32 min from Qawra. It is the only one-seat bus from the hotel area to Ta' Qali.

  • Where to board: AX ODYCY sits beside the Qawra stops; Buġibba terminus (bay 6 for the 186) is ~15 min on foot and guarantees a seat. The 186 runs via the Qawra seafront toward Mosta.
  • Return: last 186 from Ta' Qali is ~22:30 (hourly evenings); playoff days can end later — plan Bolt/eCabs back and split the fare.
  • Doors/times: the organiser emails daily door times; the venue bag policy is "rucksack maximum" — see The Event.
  • Corridor check (T-7): the old session's guides also named routes 52/56/58 on the Valletta–Ta' Qali corridor (not needed from Qawra). The tallinja app with your hotel address is the final authority.

5Harbour & Gozo ferries

  • Valletta Ferry Services — Sliema ↔ Valletta and Three Cities every 30 min (summer timetable to 31 Oct): €3.00 single / €5.00 return (€3.50/€5.20 after 19:30). Pair with the 212 for the prettiest Valletta arrival. Flag suspended in swell — check their site on the morning. vallettaferryservices.com Official
  • Gozo Highspeed — Buġibba ↔ Mġarr in ~30 min, €6.50 each way from Buġibba (Valletta €7.50). Your Gozo day-trip ferry leaves from the hotel's own bay. Flag restricted sailings 14 & 17 Oct (Middle Sea Race), Mġarr works to 31 Oct. gozohighspeed.com Official
  • Gozo Channel — Ċirkewwa ↔ Mġarr car ferry, ~every 45 min, foot passenger ≈ €4.65 return (paid on the Gozo side). Better with a rental car. gozochannel.com Official
