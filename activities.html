Gaming: what is actually open to you

You came for a CS2 tournament, so it is worth being blunt about the gaming picture near Qawra:

  • Arcades and entertainment centres are real and walk-in. The dated platform directory read lists MultiMaxx, The Eden, Falcon Archery, Gravity Malta / Jurassic and Laser Tag Malta (marked “temporarily closed” in that capture). Prices for the arcade, laser and VR products are in their own records below.
  • No public PC-gaming café was verified. The esports association’s own venue directory lists training and competition facilities, not tourist drop-in PC sessions, and no operator page offered walk-in PC play. A team bootcamp, a tournament venue and a public PC café are three different products and none of them has been confirmed here.
  • Closest casual gaming to the hotel: the Empire Cinema in Buġibba has an arcade games room under one roof with the cinema, bar and restaurant — a search-extract lead only, with hours, prices and age rules unconfirmed. See the record →
  • Best use of an event-evening: because no PC venue could be verified, the realistic options are the arcades above, a walk back along the seafront, or an outing that starts the next morning — see bike and e-bike hire in Buġibba, which has published operator prices from €15.
