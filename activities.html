Activities, events & gaming centers

What to do beyond the matches: the events that actually fall inside 13–20 October 2026, the LAN/esports venues for playing on good hardware between sessions, and the classic Malta day-out options. Gaming-venue details from directories or community reports are flagged — call before you go.

Correction applied 24 Sep 2026 The earlier version of this page put the Rolex Middle Sea Race in “early November”. The organiser (Royal Malta Yacht Club) and the Gozo Highspeed service notice both fix the 2026 start on Saturday 17 October in Grand Harbour — inside your window, on playoff Saturday. “Cristal Lagoon” has also been corrected to the Blue Lagoon (Comino).

If you want a guaranteed seat on Grand-Final weekend, ask about booking a session when you call; evening traffic in Sliema/St Julian's is heavy. Note that the tournament itself is in a studio (BLAST Studios Malta) — audience arrangements are the organiser's, not a public arena.

3Day-out activities (non-gaming)

  • Comino swim day — the Blue Lagoon. Day-trip boats from Ċirkewwa, Buġibba and Sliema; October sea ~23 °C Estimate. Boat prices are third-party and were not verified Flag — check the wind forecast and book on the day.
  • Gozo full day — Cittadella, Ġgantija, Ramla Bay, Dwejra's Inland Sea. Gozo Highspeed from Valletta €7.50 each way (45 min; no return discount shown on the official fares page; Sliema €8.50, Buġibba €6.50) or Gozo Channel from Ċirkewwa €4.65 return for a foot passenger (paid on the Gozo side). Flag Highspeed's restricted schedule on 14 & 17 Oct (Middle Sea Race) and Mġarr terminal works until 31 Oct.
  • Blue Grotto boat ride (Wied iż-Żurrieq) — morning light, weather-dependent; combine with Ħaġar Qim & Mnajdra (€10) 10 minutes away.
  • Mdina + Rabat catacombs half-day — the closest sight to the venue; see Landmarks.
  • Diving/snorkelling — dive centres in Sliema/St Julian's, Ċirkewwa and Gozo run guided shore dives all year; October is still warm. Prices not verified — book locally.
  • Harbour ferry to the Three Cities — €3 single/€5 return by day (€3.50/€5.20 after 19:30), every 30 min; the cheapest harbour cruise in Malta and a different view from the bus.
  • Paceville nightlife — the strip is 10–15 min from a Sliema/St Julian's base; the US State Department advisory (Level 1 overall) singles Paceville out for petty crime, drink-spiking and late-night fights — keep your phone and drink in hand.

4What does NOT fit the window (so you don't plan around it)

  • Summer music festivals (Isle of MTV, Glitch, Farsons Beer Festival, Malta Jazz Festival) — all June–August.
  • Carnival / Fireworks Festival / Valletta Baroque Festival — February, April, January.
  • Beach lounging on the northern coves is still fine in October, but the peak beach season is over: expect quieter bays and the last warm water of the year.
