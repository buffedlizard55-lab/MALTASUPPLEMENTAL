2Gaming centers & esports venues (play on good hardware)

Near your hotel (Qawra): there is no dedicated high-end gaming venue in Qawra itself (searched 9 Oct 2026). What exists locally: Internet cafés near the hotel (Cyber Zone, A.A, 24/7 Internet Cafe) — Cyber Zone Cafe (Triq il-Port Ruman, Qawra SPB 1701), A.A Internet Cafe (Triq Jean de la Valette, St Paul's Bay) and a 24/7 Internet Cafe (San Pawl il-Bahar SPB 3115) offer basic PC/internet access (browsing/printing, not gaming rigs) — and the 9 Ball pool & snooker hall (366 Triq It-Turisti, Qawra) is the local hangout. Flag — directory/local-guide listings; confirm on the ground. For serious PC gaming, the venues below are a bus/taxi ride away (Msida ~30–40 min, Sliema/St Julian's ~25–40 min from Qawra).

3Day-out activities (non-gaming) — incl. what's next to your hotel

Weather in your window (13–19 Oct 2026): ~20–24 °C days, cooler evenings, sea still ~23–24 °C — pack a light layer and sunscreen Estimate — climate normals, not a forecast.

  • Malta National Aquarium — at Qawra Point, ~10-min walk from AX ODYCY, next to Café del Mar. Daily 10:00–20:00; 50+ tanks, ~300 species, 12-m main-tank tunnel. Official prices (9 Oct 2026): adult (13+) €17.90 door / €16.90 online; child (4–12) €11.90/€10.90; senior €14.90/€13.90; after-4pm adult online €13.90. 45–90 min; daily talk programme (sharks 10:30, main tank 13:00/16:00, octopus 14:30, reptiles 15:00, big fish 17:00).
  • St Paul's Islands & harbour boat trips from Qawra/Buġibba — short trips to St Paul's Islands and Comino/Blue Lagoon day trips leave from the Buġibba/Qawra harbours; Flag operator prices unverified (marketplace listings: day trips ~€20–35, sunset cruises ~€30–36) — book on the day after checking the wind.
  • Beaches near the hotel (Qawra Point & Buġibba, Salina Bay, Golden Bay/Għajn Tuffieħa) — Qawra Point/Bugibba's rocky bays are 5–10 min on foot; Salina Bay's salt pans are a flat scenic walk; Golden Bay & Għajn Tuffieħa (the island's best sand) are ~20–30 min by bus (223 toward Golden Bay from Buġibba; 41/42 from Valletta). Free; lifeguards off-season in October.
  • Xemxija & St Paul's Bay heritage shore — coastal walk with Roman-baths remains, Mistra Bay promenade; free, on foot from St Paul's Bay or by bus 221/222.
  • Mosta Rotunda (Church of St Mary) — ~10 min from Qawra on bus 186 (Mosta Rotunda stop); one of Europe's largest unsupported domes; small museum admission (price unverified).
  • Comino swim day — the Blue Lagoon. Day-trip boats from Ċirkewwa, Buġibba and Sliema; October sea ~23 °C Estimate. Boat prices are third-party and were not verified Flag — check the wind forecast and book on the day.
  • Gozo full day — Cittadella, Ġgantija, Ramla Bay, Dwejra's Inland Sea. Gozo Highspeed from Valletta €7.50 each way (45 min; no return discount shown on the official fares page; Sliema €8.50, Buġibba €6.50) or Gozo Channel from Ċirkewwa €4.65 return for a foot passenger (paid on the Gozo side). Flag Highspeed's restricted schedule on 14 & 17 Oct (Middle Sea Race) and Mġarr terminal works until 31 Oct. With a Mon 19 Oct departure, a Gozo day only fits if your flight is late — otherwise swap it with another day.
  • Blue Grotto boat ride (Wied iż-Żurrieq) — morning light, weather-dependent; combine with Ħaġar Qim & Mnajdra (€10) 10 minutes away.
  • Mdina + Rabat catacombs half-day — the closest sight to the venue; see Landmarks.
  • Diving/snorkelling — dive centres in Sliema/St Julian's, Ċirkewwa and Gozo run guided shore dives all year; October is still warm. Prices not verified — book locally.
  • Harbour ferry to the Three Cities — €3 single/€5 return by day (€3.50/€5.20 after 19:30), every 30 min; the cheapest harbour cruise in Malta and a different view from the bus.
  • Paceville nightlife — the strip is 10–15 min from a Sliema/St Julian's base (further from Qawra); the US State Department advisory (Level 1 overall) singles Paceville out for petty crime, drink-spiking and late-night fights — keep your phone and drink in hand. In Qawra/Buġibba the bar strip (Bugibba Square, Tourist Street) is the lower-key local equivalent.

4What does NOT fit the window (so you don't plan around it)

  • Summer music festivals (Isle of MTV, Glitch, Farsons Beer Festival, Malta Jazz Festival) — all June–August.
  • Carnival / Fireworks Festival / Valletta Baroque Festival — February, April, January.
  • Beach lounging on the northern coves is still fine in October, but the peak beach season is over: expect quieter bays and the last warm water of the year.
