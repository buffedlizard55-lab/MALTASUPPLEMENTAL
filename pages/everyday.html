An earlier EU timeline projected Q4 2026, but that is not a launch date. Because this trip is 13–19 October, re-open the official FAQ immediately before travel and use only the official EU site if its status changes.

EES is not ETIAS

The EU says the Entry/Exit System (EES) has been fully operational at participating external borders since 10 April 2026. For visa-exempt short-stay travelers, it records entry/exit data and biometrics; a biometric passport is not required simply to cross the border, although it is needed for eligible self-service gates.

Personal checklist before departure

  • Passport valid through the required post-trip period; confirm the exact passport you will use.
  • Check ETIAS and EES notices from the official EU portal; include transit-country requirements.
  • Confirm roaming/eSIM, data access, bank fees and a backup payment method.
  • Check prescription/controlled-medicine rules and confirm travel medical/evacuation coverage.
  • Pack a Type G plug adapter; check device labels for voltage compatibility.
  • Review the weather warning and live bus/taxi details close to each outing.
