How to use the review signals

The strongest cross-platform comparisons in this check were Cafe Maroc (Google Maps 3.7/28; Tripadvisor 4.6/219), 9 Ball Cafe (4.5/533; 4.7/60), Minoa (4.9/378; 5.0/764), and Zigumar (4.6 with no Google count shown; 4.4/293). All figures are dated 9 October 2026 and are repeated on the relevant venue cards with their source links. The different counts and scores are not directly interchangeable.

Tripadvisor’s Qawra list describes a platform ranking that uses review signals and proximity, and its listings can include nearby businesses. This page therefore does not turn those ranks into a universal local-popularity claim. See the Tripadvisor Qawra restaurant list and source-log entry B-019.

Research limits

Individual Tripadvisor pages were accessible for the listed venues. Google Maps returned usable cards for only some listings and showed a limited view; where no score was captured, none is claimed. The direct Yelp and public Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit and X pages tested returned HTTP 403 in this research environment. Yelp search results pointed to US places named Malta, not Malta the country, so Yelp/social results were not used as Qawra review or popularity evidence. Details and recheck actions are recorded under Q-007 and Q-013 in the open-questions register.

All listed rating counts, prices and directory hours can change. Confirm current service with the business before travelling or budgeting. Menu links are provided where an official venue page made them available; an exact EUR spend has not been inferred from a platform price tier.
