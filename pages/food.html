At the AX ODYCY Hotel

Trattoria Riccardo

Cuisine: Italian / Pizza | Price: €15 - €30

Conveniently located inside the hotel, serving classic Italian dishes and pizzas. Great for a hearty meal before heading to the esports tournament.

Cheeky Monkey Gastropub

Cuisine: Pub Food / Burgers | Price: €15 - €25

A vibrant, popular gastropub offering burgers, snacks, and a full bar. Ideal for evening relaxation.

Minoa Mediterranean Fusion

Cuisine: Mediterranean Fine Dining | Price: €30 - €60+

Rooftop dining with spectacular sea views. Perfect for a celebratory meal or a high-end dining experience.

Nearby in Qawra

Michele's Cafe

Cuisine: Cafe / Breakfast / European | Price: €5 - €15

Highly rated on TripAdvisor, located about 0.2 miles from the hotel. Known for excellent breakfast, cakes, and coffee.

Woodhut Pub & Diner

Cuisine: International Diner | Price: €10 - €20

A family-friendly diner on Tourist Street, very close to the hotel, offering large portions and reasonable prices.
