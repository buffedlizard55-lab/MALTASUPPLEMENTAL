Gaming Centers & Esports

The Gamers Lounge

Location: Msida (near Junior College)

Malta's premier PC gaming center. Outfitted with high-end setups (Zowie, Razer, etc.) and a great spot to meet other gamers or just get some competitive matches in during your downtime.

Eden Esports Centre

Location: Eden SuperBowl, St. Julian's

Located inside the entertainment hub of St. Julian's, this center offers PC stations right next to a bowling alley and bar.

Landmarks to Visit

Mdina (The Silent City)

Location: Mdina (Very close to Ta' Qali / BLAST Arena)

The ancient fortified capital of Malta. A must-see location that feels like stepping back in time. Since it is geographically close to the event venue in Attard, it makes for a perfect pre-event excursion.

Valletta

Location: Valletta

The current capital and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Spend a day walking the grid-like streets, visiting St. John's Co-Cathedral, and taking in the views from the Upper Barrakka Gardens.
