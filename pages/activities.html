Sunday 18 October has two different rhythms

In Guardia is scheduled for 11:00 in Valletta. CDM Sundays is advertised from 18:00 to 01:00 in Qawra, but its FAQ says party tickets are only valid after 20:00. If you attend the late event, plan a taxi return rather than assuming buses will still be running.

Local and day-trip ideas

Attractions & historic places

03

Closest indoor choices

  • Malta National Aquarium: in Qawra; its current ticket page lists online and door prices, including after-16:00 entry.
  • Classic Car Collection: an indoor Qawra museum with posted daily hours and separate adult/child tickets.

Check ticket terms, current opening hours and the live talk schedule before you set out.

Historic-day options

  • Valletta: fortified capital, bastions and harbour viewpoints; museums have separate admission.
  • Mdina: a compact walled city with medieval and Baroque streets; check each interior separately.
  • Ħaġar Qim & Mnajdra: Neolithic temples with assistance/accessibility limits and a downhill footpath between sites.

For the southern temples, check current public transport, opening hours and accessibility before booking.
