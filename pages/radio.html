If you are looking to follow live sports for Kalshi prediction markets (such as the English Premier League) over the airwaves in Malta, you will primarily need a DAB+ digital radio receiver. Malta has an extensive DAB+ network covering the whole island.

TalkSport (UK)

Frequency: DAB+ (Labelled "Sports Channel" or "Talk Sport") | Language: English

Content: Live English Premier League (EPL) matches and sports talk.

Relevance for Kalshi: High. TalkSport owns audio broadcasting rights for many EPL matches. Over the weekend of October 17-18, 2026, you can tune into live match commentary here to track your sports bets.

Schedule Note: Malta (CEST) is 1 hour ahead of the UK (BST) in October. Standard Saturday EPL broadcasts on TalkSport occur at 13:30 (Early Kick-off), 16:00 (Standard), and 18:30 (Late Kick-off) local Malta time. Sunday broadcasts typically start around 15:00 and 17:30 local Malta time.

Campus FM (simulcasting BBC World Service)

Frequency: 103.7 FM & DAB+ | Language: English

Content: Educational programming and BBC World Service relays.

Relevance for Kalshi: Moderate. The BBC World Service carries "Sportsworld" on weekends, providing live updates and occasional commentary on EPL matches and other global sports.

Vibe FM

Frequency: 88.7 FM & DAB+ | Language: English / Maltese

Content: Official Radio Partner for the Malta Premier League.

Relevance for Kalshi: Low. The Malta Premier League is rarely listed on major US prediction markets, but it's available if you want to follow local sports.

Radio Sportiva

Frequency: DAB+ | Language: Italian

Content: Serie A and global sports updates.

Relevance for Kalshi: Moderate if you understand Italian and follow Serie A markets.

Note: US AM/FM only radios will not pick up TalkSport or Radio Sportiva. Ensure your travel radio has a DAB+ band, or purchase a cheap DAB+ receiver upon arrival in Malta.
