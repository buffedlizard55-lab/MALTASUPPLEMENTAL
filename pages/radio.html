Live sport on the radio

BBC schedules currently list live football commentary on 17–18 October and Sportsworld on both weekend days. This page separates the existence of a UK programme from whether the signal or stream will work in Qawra.

Access is not guaranteed

Malta's DAB+ channel list includes BBC World Service. It does not list BBC Radio 5 Live or Sports Extra. Internet listening in Malta, hotel reception and your radio's DAB+ compatibility were not verified.

How to read these times

Times in each record show both the broadcaster's UK or GMT schedule time and a Malta-local conversion. On 17–18 October the UK is on BST (UTC+1) and Malta is on CEST (UTC+2), so Malta is one hour ahead of the UK and two hours ahead of GMT. BBC schedule pages also display a browser-dependent “local time”; this guide uses the GMT metadata for Sportsworld and the UK broadcast times on the BBC match pages for football.

The conversion is a calculation from the published schedule and EU clock-change rule, not an additional broadcast guarantee.

Best verified over-the-air lead

BBC World Service appears in the Maltese DAB+ operator list, so that is the only channel in this schedule with current local terrestrial carriage evidence. The broadcaster cautions that some UK DAB receivers may use a codec that does not support Malta's DAB+ service.

BBC Radio 5 Live / Sports Extra

The match pages confirm UK programme schedules. They do not confirm an FM/DAB signal in Malta or that an online stream is available to a visitor outside the UK. Check BBC's own player and access terms from Malta before relying on it.

No alternate station's named match assignment or Malta reception was verified for the remaining trip dates.

Before you tune in

  • Re-open the BBC programme pages for time or fixture changes.
  • Convert to Malta time only after checking the current local-clock offset.
  • Test the actual receiver/stream in Malta; a listing on a national channel page does not guarantee signal at the hotel.
  • Sportsworld is a live-sport program; its listing does not promise commentary on a particular match.
