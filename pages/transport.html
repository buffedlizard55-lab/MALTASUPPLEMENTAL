Airport to AX ODYCY Hotel

From Malta International Airport (MLA) to AX ODYCY Malta (Qawra SPB 1902):

  • Taxi / Ride Share (Bolt, eCabs, Uber): ~30-40 minutes. Costs €20 - €30. Easiest and fastest option, especially with luggage.
  • Public Bus — Airport Direct TD1: ~49 minutes to Buġibba, calling at Qawra Arznell by name. Costs €3.50. Runs hourly.
  • Public Bus — cheapest, route 214: airport → Marsa → Rabat → Qawra → Buġibba. €2.00–€2.50.
  • Route X3 — withdrawn 20 April 2025 and replaced by route 214. Do not plan on it.

Hotel to BLAST Arena Studios (Attard)

The Thunderpick CS2 Finals take place at BLAST Arena Studios, located at the MFCC South Gate National Stadium, Millennium Stand, Attard ATD 4000.

  • Ride Share (Highly Recommended): Takes about 20-25 minutes from Qawra to Ta' Qali (Attard). Expect to pay €12 - €25 depending on time of day. This is the best balance of convenience, reliability, and price for an event day.
  • Public Bus — route 186, direct: this is the answer. Route 186 runs Buġibba Bay 6 → Qawra seafront → Mosta → Ta' Qali Stadium → Ta' Qali Villagg → Rabat, every 30 minutes, with no change of bus. Board at Arznell (950) or Qawra (952); roughly 24–30 minutes. Free on the €25 Explore Adult 7-Day card. First bus 06:08, last 21:37. The return does not come back along the Qawra seafront — it runs via St Paul's Bay to Buġibba Bay 1, and the last published return passing time is 21:26, so a match ending after ~20:45 is a taxi.
  • Route X3 — withdrawn 20 April 2025, and even when it ran it served Qali 2 on the main road, not the MFCC. The claim that X3 connects Qawra directly to Ta' Qali was wrong on both counts.

Getting Around Malta

Ride-Hailing Apps (eCabs, Bolt, Uber)

Convenience: High | Price: Moderate | Value: High

The most convenient way to get around Malta for short trips or events. eCabs is a local favorite, but Bolt and Uber are heavily used. No cash required.

Public Transport (Tallinja Buses)

Convenience: Moderate | Price: Very Low (€2.50) | Value: High

Malta's bus network covers the entire island. You can tap to pay with a contactless card. Best for daytime exploration, but not recommended if you are in a rush.

Rental Cars

Convenience: Low (Traffic & Parking) | Price: Moderate-High | Value: Low

Malta drives on the left (UK style). Traffic can be dense, and finding parking near Qawra or event venues can be difficult. Not recommended for a short esports trip unless you plan to explore remote parts of the island.
