On the event days

Qawra ⇄ Ta’ Qali by bus

01

Malta Public Transport publishes Route 186 at a scheduled frequency of every 30 minutes, linking Qawra with Ta’ Qali on the same route. It is the strongest bus candidate for this trip, but it is not a confirmed TWC transfer: public access, the event entrance, the best stop and the final walk still need confirmation.

i

Saturday 17 October: published Route 186 detour

MPT’s current notice schedules a Route 186 detour toward Buġibba from 19:00 to midnight, beginning 17 October, and lists stops Gholja 2 through Targa as not served. This is the direction you would need to return from Ta’ Qali toward Qawra. The exact effect on the TWC stop is not confirmed; check the live planner and keep a taxi fallback.

Before you leave the hotel

  1. Confirm that TWC has public spectator access and verify the exact entrance.
  2. For the outbound leg, check Route 186 from Qawra stop 952 (or Arznell stop 950) toward Rabat in the Tallinja planner; MPT’s current timetable shows the route every 30 minutes.
  3. Confirm which directional Stadium stop is right for the event entrance and check the pedestrian route; the MFCC address is from a BLAST guide for a different event.
  4. Plan the return separately. The normal Route 186 return toward Buġibba does not come back along Qawra’s seafront, and the posted 17 October detour may affect Ta’ Qali stops.
  5. Keep Malta Taxi or another live-quoted ride as a fallback, especially for a late finish.
