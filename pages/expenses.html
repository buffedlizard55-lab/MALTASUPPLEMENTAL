    Plan the costs you actually expect

    Each line totals quantity × unit cost. Add a known, quoted or estimated cost; record any paid/covered amount separately. You can change the date, category and status at any time.

    Daily itinerary costs

    Four blank starter lines are provided for each date. Leave an unused line blank, or add a separate line for a meal, ride, ticket or other item.

    For bus prices, enter the number of rides in “Quantity” and choose a rate only after confirming the 2026/27 fare. For one-off bookings, use quantity 1 and enter the total booking amount or leave the row as a reminder.
