Accommodation contribution

MTA's rate effective 1 July 2026 is €1.50 per night for each guest aged 18+ at the start of the visit, capped at €22.50 per person per visit. The MTA says it must be itemized separately. This planner assumes the licensed-hotel charge is your cost until you confirm otherwise.

What this total does—and does not—mean

  • All totals are in euros. The optional USD view uses only a rate you type; no live rate is fetched.
  • The environmental contribution estimate is per eligible adult, per night, capped per person per visit; untick it if confirmed covered or not applicable.
  • The bus calculator multiplies separately paid day-service rides by the currently published €2.00–€2.50 seasonal range. October's applicable rate is unresolved; a card/pass or direct service may price differently.
  • There is no airfare, hotel room quote, taxi quote, meal budget, insurance quote, exchange rate or assumed prize coverage in this worksheet.
  • Amounts are processed only in this page's browser session; refreshing resets the form.

Official price claims, date checked, and unresolved details are logged in Sources & final report and Open questions.
