## Deliverables and evidence boundaries

### A · Getting around — 18 records

The transport page and data compare public bus, taxi/ride-hailing, rental car, ferry and other island connections by convenience, published price/value, and feasibility. Route 186 was traced stop by stop in both directions from the operator timetable this pass, which settles the most consequential transport question on the site. Outbound it serves Il-Qawra (Arznell 950, Luzzu 951, Qawra 952) toward Ta’ Qali Stadium 6227, about 25–34 minutes depending on the timetable variant. The return from opposite-side Stadium stop 2194 lists **no Il-Qawra stop at all** and terminates at Buġibba Bay 1 (2527) at about 21:26–21:36, so **a second boarding is mandatory** to reach AX ODYCY and one single fare covers both legs because MPT tickets are valid two hours with free transfers. The last listed Stadium 2194 departure is **20:53**, which is the binding constraint for a late playoff finish. These remain static route-table entries, not a dated hotel connection, and the operator’s “other routes” lists mix both directions, so the onward Buġibba → Qawra connection must come from the dated planner and is not inferred here. Route 214 is an alternative to compare.

A new fare-scenario record gives the arithmetic a traveller needs the moment the itinerary lands: at €2.50 the €19 12-journey card breaks even at 8 journeys and the €25 Explore Adult 7-day at 10; at €2.00 those become 10 and 13. Because TD1 Airport Direct (€3.50) is excluded from the €25 card by that card’s own paragraph but included in the €27 Explore Flex 7-day, the €27 product is the right comparison if the airport bus is wanted. The **October 2026 single-fare season is not established by the operator’s own page** — its only dated winter window (19 Oct 2025 – 13 Jun 2026) expired before the trip and no replacement is published — so both €2.00 and €2.50 scenarios are shown and €2.50 is the conservative budget figure.

The 17 October diversion record was expanded: beyond the Route 186 notice (toward Buġibba, stops from Gholja 2 through Targa not served, 19:00–00:00 Friday/Saturday from 17 October), MPT lists a cluster of detours on the identical pattern across Routes 31, 41, 42, 45, 47, 48, 103, 202, 203, 238, 260, 280, 405 and N48 in the Mosta / Rotunda / Ta’ Qali P&R area. The diverted segment is exactly the one the 20:53 last return uses. A ticketed event also starts at 22:00 that Saturday at UNO Malta in Ta’ Qali, which is consistent with the cluster, but **MPT names no cause** and none is asserted. The site advises treating the whole Ta’ Qali bus corridor as unreliable after 19:00 on 17 October and making a live-quote taxi or ride-hailing return the primary plan that night. Airport Direct TD1 lists Il-Qawra Arznell stop 950 in both timetable directions, with route-level times; the flight-specific departure and walk to AX ODYCY still need a planner check. The current mode-comparison record does not treat an old third-party approximately €25 airport-taxi figure as an official fare. October 2026 single-fare validity, TWC entrance/walk, exact outward/return route and diversion impact remain open.

### D · Everyday life — 16 records

Power, language, currency, entry/EES/ETIAS, mobile options, safety/health and weather are sourced and qualified for date and traveler circumstances. Government of Malta and U.S. State Department sources support emergency number 112. Passport, insurance, device/carrier, medicines/customs and other personal advice is not individualized.

The forecast record now covers **all seven trip days**. Malta Met Office supplies 13–17 October (highs 23–26 °C, lows 17–19 °C, isolated showers on arrival day Tuesday 13, noticeable wind on several days); its own seven-day view did not yet reach 18–19 October when read on 11 October, so those two days come from the UK Met Office’s Valletta forecast — sunny intervals, 23 °C / 21 °C on both — whose entries are explicitly marked long-range **BETA** and are labelled as a different provider in the record. The slight model disagreement for 17 October is reported rather than reconciled. These are forecasts, not observed conditions, and no sea-state, lifeguard or warning notice was checked.

A new record, `ees-malta-implementation-conflict`, logs a genuine source conflict rather than resolving it by preference. The official EU FAQ states that progressive implementation lasted until 9 April 2026 and that from 10 April 2026 EES is fully operational at all external border crossing points of the countries using it; secondary travel-visa reporting from 16 September 2026 says Malta is among nine Schengen countries that told the Commission they cannot yet enforce EES in full. No Malta government, Identity Malta or eu-LISA statement on national status was found, and no border wait times are published, so the conflict is recorded with both source types named and the advice follows from the uncertainty: allow generous time at the border on arrival and departure day either way. The existing ETIAS record, read from the official EU site, remains the authoritative ETIAS claim and was not changed.

### E · Malta sports radio — 11 records

The page distinguishes an over-the-air service listing, an official programme schedule, local reception, online streaming, fixture data, and named-match carriage. WorldDAB lists BBC World Service on block 6A at 181.936 MHz; this technical directory is not an indoor signal test.

Official BBC episode JSON gives Sportsworld first-broadcast times with +01:00 offsets on 17 and 18 October, corresponding to 15:06 and 16:06 Malta time. The programme is general live sport, not a named-match listing; the 11 October EPL episode is precedent only. Six BBC Radio 5 Live / 5 Sports Extra episode records list UK EPL commentary during 17–18 October. **The service-label discrepancy recorded earlier is now closed:** *Premier League Football* is a BBC Radio 5 Live programme whose individual broadcasts air on either BBC Radio 5 Live or BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra, so the page slot label and the JSON `ownership.service` field were never in conflict — one names the broadcast slot, the other the owning service. The record was rewritten with all six fixtures named, their Malta-time equivalents (15:45 / 16:00 / 18:30 on Saturday 17 October and 14:55 / 15:00 / 17:30 on Sunday 18 October), and a cross-check that passed: adding one hour to each BBC UK start reproduces exactly the official Malta-time kickoffs already recorded in `epl-fixtures-trip-weekend-17-19-oct`. Those Malta times fall on the two playoff days, so live English football commentary on a UK service overlaps the CS2 schedule. These UK records still do not establish Malta reception, BBC Sounds access, BBC World Service carriage, or a Maltese frequency.

One radio item is deliberately **absent from the data**: a search-index snapshot of the BBC Radio 5 Live Sport upcoming-broadcasts page suggested studio programmes on Fri 16 Oct 19:00, Sat 17 Oct 12:00, Sun 18 Oct 12:00 and 16:00, and Mon 19 Oct 19:00, but the page could not be fetched directly this session.
