## Source-type rules

- **official** — venue, operator, broadcaster, government, league, organizer, or first-party business page.
- **map** — map/listing platform (Google Maps and similar); snapshot information is dated.
- **review** — review platform or independently identified review aggregate; review counts/ratings are point-in-time signals, not guaranteed quality.
- **social** — public posts/accounts on social platforms; only cite a post if it was actually viewable and attributable.

Do not describe a source as checked if it could not be opened. Search snippets are leads, not verification. Keep unresolved access and conflicting information in `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
