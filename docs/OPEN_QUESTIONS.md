**Resolved at the rule level on 11 October 2026 (second session); the invoice figure remains open.** The Malta Tourism Authority’s guidelines for the 1 July 2026 change state that the contribution is €1.50 per night for each guest aged 18 or over at the start of the visit, capped at €22.50 per eligible person per visit; it is chargeable per night the accommodation is made available, and must be disclosed separately from accommodation fees on a fiscal receipt. For the supplied six-night window the arithmetic is €9.00 per eligible adult. That is a calculation from published rules, not a quoted charge: the amount to budget depends on the nights AX ODYCY bills and on each guest’s age at check-in, so enter the invoice figure. ## OQ-25 — MTA “15-Night Threshold” wording

**Resolved 11 October 2026 (second session).** Reading the MTA guidelines PDF in full shows that the €22.50 per-person cap is produced by fifteen chargeable nights (15 × €1.50), and that the “15-night” sentence describes when the reporting obligation and the nil-charge treatment of unlicensed-interval nights apply to long stays — it is a cap/administration mechanism, not an exemption for short stays and not an extra charge. A short stay is therefore charged €1.50 per night per guest aged 18+, up to the cap. Sources read 11 October 2026: https://mta.com.mt/docs/Eco-Contribution-Accommodation-Guidelines-2026.pdf (rate, cap, “visit” definition, receipt rules); https://mta.com.mt/services/environmental-contribution/. The only remaining question is the invoice figure (OQ-23).
