3Maltese classics — the food to actually try

National snack

Pastizzi

Diamond-shaped flaky pastry filled with ricotta or mushy peas, ~€0.50–1.50 each, sold from early morning at every pastizzerija. Warm is best. Is-Serkin (Crystal Palace Bar) — the cult name — is in Rabat, by Mdina's gate, not in Valletta.

UNESCO-listed

Ftira

Ring-shaped Maltese bread topped with tuna, sardines, tomato & capers — ftira was added to UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2020. Try Nenu the Artisan Baker (Valletta); Qormi's ~50 bakeries are a pilgrimage.

National dish

Stuffat tal-Fenek

Rabbit stewed in red wine, garlic and tomato — the emblem of Maltese cuisine, best in village restaurants (Mġarr and Rabat are the classic rabbit villages; Gululu in St Julian's is the awarded in-town option).

Also order

Bragioli, ġbejniet, imqaret, lampuki

Bragioli: thin beef slices rolled round a bacon/egg/breadcrumb stuffing and braised. Ġbejniet: small sheep's/goat's-milk cheeselets, fresh or peppered. Imqaret: deep-fried date-filled pastries (stalls outside Valletta's City Gate). Lampuki (dorado): October is peak season — order lampuki pie or grilled lampuki anywhere that week. Plus Kinnie (bitter-orange & herb soda), bigilla (broad-bean dip) and ħobż biż-żejt (oil-and-tomato bread).

Verified 23 Sep 2026 maltadventures.com Maltese-cuisine guide, jesseatstheworld.com, AFAR's "best food in Malta" — consistent on dishes and named spots; dish descriptions corrected 24 Sep 2026 (ġbejniet are cheeselets, not dumplings; imqaret contain dates only; Is-Serkin is in Rabat). 5Eating on the $30/day budget

$30/day in Malta is workable but tight A sit-down dinner in Sliema or St Julian's alone can run €20–30 before drinks. To make $30/day (≈€26) work:
  • Breakfast: pastizzi + coffee (~€3–5)
  • Lunch: ftira/tuna or pastizzerija-style meal, or a café plate (~€6–10)
  • Dinner: share or take from a mid-range trattoria's lunch menu when possible; the €15–20 mains are where the budget lives or dies
Budget-compatible picks from the awarded lists: Galea's Kitchen (Valletta, open 09:00–23:00), Trattoria da Manuele (Mġarr, lowest price tier, closed Mon — pair it with the Ta' Ħaġrat/Skorba temples), The Village Fork (Best Value award), Gululu for one proper Maltese dinner, and Busy Bee for breakfast pastries. October is lampuki season, so the fish special is usually the cheapest fish on the board. The Top-10 fine-dining rooms are a one-night splurge, not a weekly rhythm.
