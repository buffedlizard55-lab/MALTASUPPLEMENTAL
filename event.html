Confirmed from the event’s own pages — read this first
  • The venue address is confirmed for this event, not borrowed from a different BLAST event: BLAST Arena Studios Malta at the MFCC South Gate National Stadium, ATD 4000 Attard, pinned at 35.8957343, 14.4168839 on the ticketing page’s own map links. The studio sits inside the Ta’ Qali stadium complex — which is why the Ta’ Qali Stadium bus stops are the right target.
  • Spectator attendance exists and is listed as free. The event’s ticketing/registration page runs Wednesday 14 – Sunday 18 October, is labelled Free with a “Reserve a spot” action, and states “In person” and “ages 18+”. Bring government-issued photo ID. A listing is not proof of current inventory or of a completed reservation by you — reserve before travelling.
  • Daily times are published for three of the five days: Wed 14 and Thu 15 October — check-in 09:30, games 10:30, day listed to 22:30; Fri 16 October — check-in 14:00, games 15:00, day listed to 21:00. Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 sit behind a control that did not expand. The listing header says 22:00 while the Wed/Thu rows say 22:30 — confirm the finish with the organiser.
  • The listed late finish drives the transport plan. A 22:30 venue day is later than the last recorded bus departure from the Ta’ Qali Stadium stop (20:53), that return direction serves no Il-Qawra stop at all (it terminates at Buġibba Bay 1), and no night route was verified to reach Ta’ Qali or Attard. Plan a taxi or ride-hailing return for the event evenings — see the return-gap analysis and the transfer record.
  • One thing could still fix the return, and one ceiling to compare against. MPT runs TQ-numbered special event services out of Ta’ Qali and TQ1’s corridor runs through Qawra at the €3.00 Special Services fare — but no timetable and no TWC notice were published, so check the operator’s service updates daily during 14–18 October. For the taxi leg, Transport Malta publishes maximum meter rates as a comparison ceiling (that page was last updated in April 2022).
  • 13 October has no public matches — it is the organiser’s media day and the arrival day of this hotel stay: use it to reserve the ticket, buy a travel card and walk the bus stops.

Event-day plan in one glance

Planning sequence from the records above — a checklist, not a booked itinerary.

  1. Reserve your free spot on the official ticketing listing and read the FAQ answers on the page (parking, per-day validity, food and drink, transfer). Carry photo ID — entry is 18+.
  2. Go out by bus if it suits you: Route 186 from the Qawra seafront stops (Arznell 950 / Luzzu 951 / Qawra 952) toward Rabat, alighting at Ta’ Qali Stadium 6227. It clears the 09:30 check-in. Compare Route 214 and the planner for your date.
  3. Fix the return before you go: pre-book or app-book a taxi for the listed 22:30 (Wed/Thu) and 21:00 (Fri) finishes, or leave early to catch the 20:53 variant from Stadium stop 2194 — which still only reaches Buġibba, not Qawra. Watch the service updates for a TQ special service.
  4. Saturday 17 October is the highest-risk evening: the Route 186 direction toward Buġibba is on a published evening detour that omits stops from Gholja 2 through Targa (MPT’s two pages show 19:00–00:00 and 20:00–24:00; plan for the union). Confirm pickup arrangements at the venue before the playoffs.
  5. Eat before or after, not during: the listing does not say whether food and drink are sold inside. Nearby Qawra/Buġibba options and the Mdina/Mosta stops on the way back are in the Food & cafés guide.
