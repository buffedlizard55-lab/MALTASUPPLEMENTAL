2Venue

  • BLAST Arena Studios (the organiser's and community listings' name; “BLAST Studios Malta” in the press releases and BLAST's own attendee guide) inside the Malta Fairs & Conventions Centre, Ta' Qali, ATD 4000, Ħ'Attard. Address verbatim from BLAST's attendee guide: “Malta Fairs & Conventions Centre Ta Qali, ATD 4000 Ħ'Attard”.
  • Location: central Malta, ~9 km / ~25–30 min by bus (Route 186) from your AX ODYCY in Qawra; the Ta' Qali National Crafts Village is right next door — a low-key option for event-day free time. The US Embassy Malta is in the same Ta' Qali complex (Ta' Qali National Park Street, Attard ATD 4000).
  • The same hall hosted BLAST Bounty (30 July–2 Aug 2026); BLAST's attendee guide from that event is the best practical preview of the building: bag checks at the door (rucksack size max), no food/drink, no detachable-lens cameras, no drones, no smoking/vaping in the studio.

4Format & prize pool

  • Group stage: 2 groups of 4 in GSL (double-elimination) format, best-of-3; the top two teams from each group advance to the playoffs (4 teams in total).
  • Playoffs: 4-team single elimination, all Bo3 except the Grand Final Bo5; 3rd-place decider.
  • Tournament operator: GAM3RS_X; data partner GRID Esports. Official rulebook is linked from the format page (Google Drive).

6Context — the TWC series

  • Fourth edition of an independent, bookmaker-backed CS2 tournament; data partner GRID since the start.
  • 2025 Finals were also in Malta (15–19 Oct 2025): FURIA came back from 0–2 down to beat NAVI 3–2.
  • 2026 season ran regional EU/NA/SA online series in May–June before the closed qualifier picked the final slot.
  • TWC 2027 has already been announced; Hotspawn describes itself as official media partner for 2026 and 2027, and the 16 Sep 2026 release says “Hotspawn returns as a Presenting Partner.”

7What your prize ticket means in practice

  • One ticket to the TWC 2026 Finals at BLAST Arena Studios (exact day-coverage to be confirmed by Hotspawn — it is one of the items on the written-confirmation list).
  • Return flights to Malta + accommodation are part of the same package (Terms said 13–20 Oct; your confirmed booking is AX ODYCY, Qawra, 13–19 Oct).
  • Excluded by the Terms: meals, transfers, insurance, extra baggage, passport/visa fees, personal spending. Everything on Costs & Budget is what you pay.
  • Non-transferable, no cash value — if the prize falls through, there is no substitute (see Hotspawn & Prize).
