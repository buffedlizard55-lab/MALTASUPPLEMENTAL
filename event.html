Before leaving the hotel

  1. Access: open your reservation and confirm day validity, admission rules and the attendee entrance with the organizer.
  2. Outbound: plan from the hotel entrance to that gate at the actual required arrival time. Check stops, walks and live notices.
  3. Return: check a separate journey after the expected finish. Compare a live ride quote if no suitable bus is confirmed; agree the pickup location.
  4. Food: confirm last food orders rather than relying on a venue’s closing time.
  5. Costs: save each receipt once in the planner. A listing is not a personal ticket or paid booking.
