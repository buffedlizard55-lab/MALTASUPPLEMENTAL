Confirmed on 11 October 2026 — read this first
  • The venue address is now confirmed for this specific event, not just inferred from a different BLAST event: BLAST Arena Studios Malta, MFCC South Gate National Stadium, ATD 4000 Attard, pinned at 35.8957343, 14.4168839.
  • In-person spectator attendance exists. A live ticketing/registration page runs Wednesday 14 – Sunday 18 October, lists the event as “In person” and “ages 18+”, and its own ticket block displays “Free” with a “Reserve a spot” action under a “Sales end soon” notice; HLTV independently reports the tickets are free. Bring photo ID, reserve before you travel, and confirm the total at checkout. No reservation is held by this guide.
  • Daily times are published for three of the five days: Wed 14 and Thu 15 October check-in 09:30 / games 10:30 (day listed to 22:30); Fri 16 October check-in 14:00 / games 15:00 (day listed to 21:00). Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 did not render.
  • The late finish drives the transport plan. A listed 22:30 finish on the two group-stage days is after the last recorded bus departure from the Ta' Qali Stadium stop (20:53), and that return direction serves no Il-Qawra stop at all — it terminates at Buġibba Bay 1, so even catching it means a mandatory second boarding. The return therefore needs a taxi, ride-hailing or pre-arranged lift. See the return-gap analysis and the transfer record.
  • One thing could still fix the return. MPT runs TQ-numbered special event services out of Ta' Qali, and TQ1's corridor runs through St Paul's Bay and Qawra to Buġibba at the €3.00 Special Services fare. It showed "Timetable not available!" when checked and no TWC notice was found, so check the operator's service updates daily during 14–18 October. For the taxi leg, Transport Malta publishes maximum meter rates — useful as a ceiling to compare a live quote against, though that page was last updated in April 2022.
  • 13 October has no public matches. That is the organiser’s media day, and it is the arrival day of this hotel stay — use it to settle in and pre-book return transport.
